영역을 극좌표로 나타낸다. 원 \(x^2+y^2=2y\)는 \(r=2\sin\theta\), 원 \(x^2+y^2=1\)은 \(r=1\)이다. 두 원의 교점은 \(2\sin\theta=1\), 즉 \(\theta=\pi/6,\ 5\pi/6\)에서 생긴다. 따라서

\[D=\Big\{(r,\theta):\ \tfrac{\pi}{6}\le\theta\le\tfrac{5\pi}{6},\ 1\le r\le 2\sin\theta\Big\},\qquad \rho=r.\]

질량

\[\begin{aligned}m&=\iint_D\rho\,dA=\int_{\pi/6}^{5\pi/6}\!\!\int_1^{2\sin\theta} r\cdot r\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_{\pi/6}^{5\pi/6}\frac{8\sin^3\theta-1}{3}\,d\theta.\end{aligned}\]

\(\displaystyle\int\sin^3\theta\,d\theta=-\cos\theta+\tfrac13\cos^3\theta\)이므로 \(\displaystyle\int_{\pi/6}^{5\pi/6}\sin^3\theta\,d\theta=\frac{3\sqrt3}{8}\cdot 2=\frac{3\sqrt3}{4}\)이다. 따라서

\[m=\frac13\Big(8\cdot\frac{3\sqrt3}{4}-\frac{2\pi}{3}\Big)=2\sqrt3-\frac{2\pi}{9}.\]

모멘트

영역과 밀도 \(\rho\)가 모두 \(y\)축에 대해 대칭(\(x\to-x\))이고 \(x\)는 기함수이므로 \(M_y=\iint_D x\rho\,dA=0\), 즉 \(\bar x=0\)이다.

\[\begin{aligned}M_x&=\iint_D y\rho\,dA=\int_{\pi/6}^{5\pi/6}\!\!\int_1^{2\sin\theta} (r\sin\theta)\,r\cdot r\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_{\pi/6}^{5\pi/6}\sin\theta\Big(4\sin^4\theta-\tfrac14\Big)d\theta\\&=4\int_{\pi/6}^{5\pi/6}\sin^5\theta\,d\theta-\tfrac14\int_{\pi/6}^{5\pi/6}\sin\theta\,d\theta.\end{aligned}\]

\(\displaystyle\int\sin^5\theta\,d\theta=-\cos\theta+\tfrac23\cos^3\theta-\tfrac15\cos^5\theta\)이므로 \(\displaystyle\int_{\pi/6}^{5\pi/6}\sin^5\theta\,d\theta=\frac{49\sqrt3}{80}\)이고, \(\displaystyle\int_{\pi/6}^{5\pi/6}\sin\theta\,d\theta=\sqrt3\)이다. 따라서

\[M_x=\frac{49\sqrt3}{20}-\frac{\sqrt3}{4}=\frac{11\sqrt3}{5}.\]

질량중심

\[\bar y=\frac{M_x}{m}=\frac{11\sqrt3/5}{2\sqrt3-2\pi/9}=\frac{99\sqrt3}{10\,(9\sqrt3-\pi)}\approx1.378.\]

답. 질량중심은 \(\left(0,\ \dfrac{99\sqrt3}{10(9\sqrt3-\pi)}\right)\approx(0,\,1.378)\)이다. (\(m=2\sqrt3-\tfrac{2\pi}{9}\))

\(s^2z_{ss}\)

두 적분의 피적분함수는 \(r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta=dV\)이므로 둘 다 부피(피적분함수 1)이다. 영역을 부등식으로 쓴다. 두 경우 모두 \(0\le\theta\le\pi/2\)이고 \(z\ge0\)이다.

  • 첫째: \(r^2(\cos^2\theta+2\sin^2\theta)\le4\), 즉 \(x^2+2y^2\le4\)이고 \(0\le z\le r\sin\theta=y\).
  • 둘째: \(x^2+2y^2\ge4\), \(r\le2\)이고 \(0\le z\le\sqrt{4-r^2}\), 즉 구 \(x^2+y^2+z^2\le4\) 안.

\(y\ge0\)일 때 \(y\le\sqrt{4-r^2}\iff x^2+2y^2\le4\)이므로, 두 조각은 \(z\le\min\{y,\sqrt{4-r^2}\}\)를 \(x^2+2y^2\)의 크기로 나눈 것이다. 즉 합집합은

\[E=\{(x,y,z):\ x,y,z\ge0,\ x^2+y^2+z^2\le4,\ z\le y\}.\]

구면좌표 \((\rho,\theta,\phi)\)에서 \(z=\rho\cos\phi\), \(y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta\)이므로 \(z\le y\iff\cot\phi\le\sin\theta\), \(z\ge0\iff\phi\le\pi/2\)이다. \(\phi\in(0,\pi/2]\)에서 \(\cot\)은 감소하므로 \(\cot\phi\le\sin\theta\iff\phi\ge\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)\). 따라서

\[0\le\theta\le\tfrac\pi2,\qquad \cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)\le\phi\le\tfrac\pi2,\qquad 0\le\rho\le2.\]

\(\phi\)의 구간이 \(\theta\)에 의존하므로 \(\phi\)를 \(\theta\)보다 안쪽에서 적분한다(순서 \(d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\)). \(dV=\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\)이므로

\[\int_0^{\pi/2}\!\int_{\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)}^{\pi/2}\!\int_0^{2}\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta.\]

같은 영역을 \(\phi\)를 바깥에 두고 쓸 수도 있다. \(\sin\theta\ge\cot\phi\)가 가능하려면 \(\cot\phi\le1\), 즉 \(\phi\ge\pi/4\)이고, 이때 \(\sin^{-1}(\cot\phi)\le\theta\le\pi/2\)이므로

\[\int_{\pi/4}^{\pi/2}\!\int_{\sin^{-1}(\cot\phi)}^{\pi/2}\!\int_0^{2}\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\theta\,d\phi.\]

답. \(\displaystyle\int_{\theta=0}^{\pi/2}\!\int_{\phi=\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)}^{\pi/2}\!\int_{\rho=0}^{2}\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\) (\(\phi\)를 바깥에 둔 위의 두 번째 형태도 같은 적분이다.)

(b)

(a)의 적분에서 \(\int_0^2\rho^2\,d\rho=\tfrac83\)이고, \(\phi\)에 대한 적분은 \(\big[-\cos\phi\big]_{\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)}^{\pi/2}=\cos\big(\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)\big)\)이다. \(\cot\alpha=\sin\theta\) \((0\lt\alpha\le\pi/2)\)이면 \(\cos\alpha=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\sqrt{1+\sin^2\theta}}\)이므로

\[\begin{aligned}V&=\frac83\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{\sin\theta}{\sqrt{1+\sin^2\theta}}\,d\theta=\frac83\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{\sin\theta}{\sqrt{2-\cos^2\theta}}\,d\theta\\&\overset{u=\cos\theta}{=}\frac83\int_0^1\frac{du}{\sqrt{2-u^2}}=\frac83\Big[\sin^{-1}\frac{u}{\sqrt2}\Big]_0^1\\&=\frac83\cdot\frac\pi4=\frac{2\pi}{3}.\end{aligned}\]

검산. 평면 \(z=y\)에 대한 대칭 \(y\leftrightarrow z\)는 구와 제1팔분공간을 보존하고 \(\{z\le y\}\)와 \(\{y\le z\}\)를 서로 바꾸므로, \(E\)의 부피는 제1팔분공간 구의 부피 \(\tfrac18\cdot\tfrac{4}{3}\pi\cdot2^3=\tfrac{4\pi}{3}\)의 절반 \(\tfrac{2\pi}{3}\)이다.

답. \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\)

채점 포인트 · 자주 하는 실수

  • 두 반복적분이 \(x,y,z\ge0\), \(x^2+y^2+z^2\le4\), \(z\le y\)인 하나의 입체임을 \(x^2+2y^2=4\)(평면 \(z=y\)와 구의 교선의 사영)로 설명한다.
  • \(z\le y\iff\cot\phi\le\sin\theta\)로 \(\phi\)의 범위를 구한다. \(\phi\)의 구간이 \(\theta\)에 의존하므로 순서는 \(d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\)이며(경계와 적분 순서가 맞아야 한다), 야코비안 \(\rho^2\sin\phi\)를 빠뜨리지 않는다.
  • 대칭(\(y\leftrightarrow z\))으로 답 \(2\pi/3\)을 검산할 수 있다.

(a)

Both integrands are \(r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta=dV\), so the integral is the volume of the region. Write the region by inequalities; in both pieces \(0\le\theta\le\pi/2\) and \(z\ge0\).

  • First: \(r^2(\cos^2\theta+2\sin^2\theta)\le4\), i.e. \(x^2+2y^2\le4\), and \(0\le z\le r\sin\theta=y\).
  • Second: \(x^2+2y^2\ge4\), \(r\le2\), and \(0\le z\le\sqrt{4-r^2}\), i.e. inside the sphere \(x^2+y^2+z^2\le4\).

For \(y\ge0\), \(y\le\sqrt{4-r^2}\iff x^2+2y^2\le4\). So on the first piece the plane \(z=y\) lies below the sphere, and on the second the sphere lies below the plane; the union is \(z\le\min\{y,\sqrt{4-r^2}\}\):

\[E=\{(x,y,z):\ x,y,z\ge0,\ x^2+y^2+z^2\le4,\ z\le y\}.\]

In spherical coordinates \((\rho,\theta,\phi)\), \(z=\rho\cos\phi\) and \(y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta\), so \(z\le y\iff\cot\phi\le\sin\theta\), and \(z\ge0\iff\phi\le\pi/2\). On \((0,\pi/2]\) \(\cot\) is decreasing, so \(\cot\phi\le\sin\theta\iff\phi\ge\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)\):

\[0\le\theta\le\tfrac\pi2,\qquad \cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)\le\phi\le\tfrac\pi2,\qquad 0\le\rho\le2.\]

The \(\phi\)-limits depend on \(\theta\), so \(\phi\) is integrated inside \(\theta\) (order \(d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\)). With \(dV=\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\):

\[\int_0^{\pi/2}\!\int_{\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)}^{\pi/2}\!\int_0^{2}\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta.\]

The same region can also be described with \(\phi\) outside: \(\sin\theta\ge\cot\phi\) is possible only if \(\cot\phi\le1\), i.e. \(\phi\ge\pi/4\), and then \(\sin^{-1}(\cot\phi)\le\theta\le\pi/2\), giving

\[\int_{\pi/4}^{\pi/2}\!\int_{\sin^{-1}(\cot\phi)}^{\pi/2}\!\int_0^{2}\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\theta\,d\phi.\]

Answer. \(\displaystyle\int_{\theta=0}^{\pi/2}\!\int_{\phi=\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)}^{\pi/2}\!\int_{\rho=0}^{2}\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\) (the second form above, with \(\phi\) outside, is the same integral).

(b)

In the integral of (a), \(\int_0^2\rho^2\,d\rho=\tfrac83\), and the \(\phi\)-integral is \(\big[-\cos\phi\big]_{\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)}^{\pi/2}=\cos\big(\cot^{-1}(\sin\theta)\big)\). If \(\cot\alpha=\sin\theta\) with \(0\lt\alpha\le\pi/2\), then \(\cos\alpha=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\sqrt{1+\sin^2\theta}}\). Hence

\[\begin{aligned}V&=\frac83\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{\sin\theta}{\sqrt{1+\sin^2\theta}}\,d\theta=\frac83\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{\sin\theta}{\sqrt{2-\cos^2\theta}}\,d\theta\\&\overset{u=\cos\theta}{=}\frac83\int_0^1\frac{du}{\sqrt{2-u^2}}=\frac83\Big[\sin^{-1}\frac{u}{\sqrt2}\Big]_0^1\\&=\frac83\cdot\frac\pi4=\frac{2\pi}{3}.\end{aligned}\]

Check. The reflection \(y\leftrightarrow z\) preserves the ball and the first octant and swaps \(\{z\le y\}\) with \(\{y\le z\}\). So \(E\) has half the volume of the first-octant part of the ball, \(\tfrac18\cdot\tfrac43\pi\cdot2^3=\tfrac{4\pi}3\), namely \(\tfrac{2\pi}{3}\).

Answer. \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\)

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • Explain that the two iterated integrals form one solid: \(x,y,z\ge0\), \(x^2+y^2+z^2\le4\), \(z\le y\); the curve \(x^2+2y^2=4\) is the projection of the intersection of the plane \(z=y\) with the sphere.
  • Convert \(z\le y\) to \(\cot\phi\le\sin\theta\) to get the \(\phi\)-range. Because the \(\phi\)-limits depend on \(\theta\), the order is \(d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\) (limits and order must match); do not drop the factor \(\rho^2\sin\phi\).
  • The symmetry \(y\leftrightarrow z\) gives a quick check of the answer \(2\pi/3\).
