한국어 · 번역

밀도함수가 \(\rho(x,y)=y\)일 때, 다음 타원으로 둘러싸인 얇은 판의 질량중심을 구하라.

\[x^2+4(y-1)^2=4\]

\(x^2+4(y-1)^2=4\)는 \(\dfrac{x^2}{4}+(y-1)^2=1\)이므로 중심 \((0,1)\), \(x\)방향 반지름 \(2\), \(y\)방향 반지름 \(1\)인 타원이다. 둘러싸인 영역을 \(D\)라 하면 \(D\)에서 \(0\le y\le2\)이므로 밀도 \(\rho=y\ge0\)이다(그림).

변환 \(x=2r\cos\theta,\ y=1+r\sin\theta\) (\(0\le r\le1,\ 0\le\theta\le2\pi\))는 \(D\)를 덮는다. 야코비안은

\[\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(r,\theta)}=\begin{vmatrix}2\cos\theta&-2r\sin\theta\\ \sin\theta&r\cos\theta\end{vmatrix}=2r\]

이므로 \(dA=2r\,dr\,d\theta\)이다.

질량.

\[\begin{aligned}m&=\iint_D y\,dA=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^1(1+r\sin\theta)\,2r\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{2\pi}\Bigl(1+\tfrac23\sin\theta\Bigr)d\theta=2\pi.\end{aligned}\]

\(y\)축에 관한 모멘트. \(D\)는 \(y\)축에 대해 대칭이고(\(x\to-x\)), 밀도 \(y\)는 \(x\)에 의존하지 않으므로 피적분함수 \(xy\)는 \(x\)에 관해 홀함수이다. 따라서 \(M_y=\iint_D xy\,dA=0\)이고 \(\bar x=0\)이다.

\(x\)축에 관한 모멘트.

\[\begin{aligned}M_x&=\iint_D y^2\,dA=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^1(1+r\sin\theta)^2\,2r\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^1\bigl(2r+4r^2\sin\theta+2r^3\sin^2\theta\bigr)\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{2\pi}\Bigl(1+\tfrac43\sin\theta+\tfrac12\sin^2\theta\Bigr)d\theta\\&=2\pi+0+\tfrac12\cdot\pi=\tfrac{5\pi}{2}.\end{aligned}\]

따라서 \(\bar y=\dfrac{M_x}{m}=\dfrac{5\pi/2}{2\pi}=\dfrac54\)이다.

답. 질량중심 \((\bar x,\bar y)=\bigl(0,\tfrac54\bigr)\).

채점 포인트 · 자주 하는 실수

  • 타원을 \(x=2r\cos\theta,\ y=1+r\sin\theta\)로 바꾸고 야코비안 \(2r\)을 곱한다(\(r\)만 곱하면 \(m\)과 \(M_x\)가 모두 틀린다).
  • \(\bar x=0\)은 대칭 근거(영역이 \(y\)축 대칭, 밀도가 \(x\)에 무관)를 써서 처리한다.
  • \(\bar y=M_x/m\), \(M_x=\iint_D y\,\rho\,dA\)이다. \(M_x\)와 \(M_y\)를 바꿔 쓰는 실수가 잦다. 밀도가 위쪽에서 크므로 \(\bar y>1\)인지 검산한다.
English · 원문

Find the center of mass of the lamina bounded by the ellipse

\[x^2+4(y-1)^2=4\]

if the density function is given by \(\rho(x,y)=y\).

Since \(x^2+4(y-1)^2=4\) is \(\dfrac{x^2}{4}+(y-1)^2=1\), the ellipse has center \((0,1)\), semi-axis \(2\) in the \(x\)-direction and \(1\) in the \(y\)-direction. Call the enclosed region \(D\). On \(D\), \(0\le y\le2\), so the density \(\rho=y\) is nonnegative (figure).

The transformation \(x=2r\cos\theta,\ y=1+r\sin\theta\) (\(0\le r\le1,\ 0\le\theta\le2\pi\)) covers \(D\), with Jacobian

\[\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(r,\theta)}=\begin{vmatrix}2\cos\theta&-2r\sin\theta\\ \sin\theta&r\cos\theta\end{vmatrix}=2r,\]

so \(dA=2r\,dr\,d\theta\).

Mass.

\[\begin{aligned}m&=\iint_D y\,dA=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^1(1+r\sin\theta)\,2r\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{2\pi}\Bigl(1+\tfrac23\sin\theta\Bigr)d\theta=2\pi.\end{aligned}\]

Moment about the \(y\)-axis. \(D\) is symmetric about the \(y\)-axis (\(x\to-x\)) and the density \(y\) does not depend on \(x\), so the integrand \(xy\) is odd in \(x\). Hence \(M_y=\iint_D xy\,dA=0\) and \(\bar x=0\).

Moment about the \(x\)-axis.

\[\begin{aligned}M_x&=\iint_D y^2\,dA=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^1(1+r\sin\theta)^2\,2r\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^1\bigl(2r+4r^2\sin\theta+2r^3\sin^2\theta\bigr)\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{2\pi}\Bigl(1+\tfrac43\sin\theta+\tfrac12\sin^2\theta\Bigr)d\theta\\&=2\pi+0+\tfrac12\cdot\pi=\tfrac{5\pi}{2}.\end{aligned}\]

Therefore \(\bar y=\dfrac{M_x}{m}=\dfrac{5\pi/2}{2\pi}=\dfrac54\).

Answer. Center of mass \((\bar x,\bar y)=\bigl(0,\tfrac54\bigr)\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • Use \(x=2r\cos\theta,\ y=1+r\sin\theta\) and multiply by the Jacobian \(2r\) (using just \(r\) makes both \(m\) and \(M_x\) wrong).
  • Justify \(\bar x=0\) by symmetry: the region is symmetric about the \(y\)-axis and the density does not depend on \(x\).
  • \(\bar y=M_x/m\) with \(M_x=\iint_D y\,\rho\,dA\); swapping \(M_x\) and \(M_y\) is a frequent slip. Since the density is larger near the top, check that \(\bar y>1\).
22 기말① 2번 닫힌 공간곡선 위의 선적분A line integral around a closed space curve10 pts §16.2§16.3
한국어 · 번역

\(\mathbf F=(x-\sin x)\,\mathbf i+(y^2+e^y)\,\mathbf j+(x+z)\,\mathbf k\)이고 \(C\)가 다음 곡선(\(0\le t\le2\pi\))일 때 선적분 \(\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r\)를 구하라.

\[\mathbf r(t)=(1+\sin(20t))\cos t\,\mathbf i+(1+\sin(20t))\sin t\,\mathbf j+\cos(20t)\,\mathbf k\]

먼저 \(C\)가 닫힌곡선임을 확인한다. \(\mathbf r(0)=\langle1,0,1\rangle\)이고, \(\sin40\pi=0\)이므로

\[\mathbf r(2\pi)=\langle\cos2\pi,\ \sin2\pi,\ \cos40\pi\rangle=\langle1,0,1\rangle\]

이다. 이제 \(\mathbf F\)를 기울기 벡터장과 나머지로 나눈다.

\[\begin{aligned}\mathbf F&=\nabla f+x\,\mathbf k,\\ f(x,y,z)&=\frac{x^2}{2}+\cos x+\frac{y^3}{3}+e^y+\frac{z^2}{2}.\end{aligned}\]

실제로 \(f_x=x-\sin x\), \(f_y=y^2+e^y\), \(f_z=z\)이고, \(\mathbf k\)성분에서 남는 부분이 \(x\)이다. (\(\mathbf F\) 자체는 보존적이 아니다: \(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F=\langle0,-1,0\rangle\neq\mathbf 0\).)

선적분의 기본정리에 의해 닫힌곡선 위에서 \(\displaystyle\oint_C\nabla f\cdot d\mathbf r=f(\mathbf r(2\pi))-f(\mathbf r(0))=0\)이다. 남은 항에는 \(x=(1+\sin20t)\cos t\), \(dz=-20\sin20t\,dt\)를 대입한다.

\[\begin{aligned}\oint_C x\,dz&=\int_0^{2\pi}(1+\sin20t)\cos t\,(-20\sin20t)\,dt\\&=-20\int_0^{2\pi}\cos t\,\sin20t\,dt\\&\quad-20\int_0^{2\pi}\cos t\,\sin^2 20t\,dt.\end{aligned}\]

곱을 합으로 바꾸면

\[\begin{aligned}\cos t\,\sin20t&=\tfrac12(\sin21t+\sin19t),\\ \cos t\,\sin^2 20t&=\tfrac12\cos t-\tfrac14(\cos41t+\cos39t)\end{aligned}\]

이다(\(\sin^2 20t=\tfrac12(1-\cos40t)\) 이용). 자연수 \(n\)에 대해 \(\int_0^{2\pi}\sin nt\,dt=\int_0^{2\pi}\cos nt\,dt=0\)이므로 두 적분은 모두 \(0\)이다. 따라서

\[\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\oint_C\nabla f\cdot d\mathbf r+\oint_C x\,dz=0+0=0.\]

답. \(0\).

채점 포인트 · 자주 하는 실수

  • \(\mathbf r(0)=\mathbf r(2\pi)\)를 계산해 \(C\)가 닫힌곡선임을 먼저 보인다.
  • \(\mathbf F\)는 보존적이 아니므로(\(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\neq\mathbf 0\)) "닫힌곡선이니 \(0\)"이라고 바로 쓰면 근거가 틀린다. 보존적인 부분 \(\nabla f\)와 남는 \(x\,\mathbf k\)를 분리한다.
  • \(\oint_C x\,dz\)는 곱→합 공식(삼각함수의 직교성)으로 \(0\)임을 보인다. 전체를 직접 매개화하면 계산이 매우 길어진다.
English · 원문

Let \(\mathbf F=(x-\sin x)\,\mathbf i+(y^2+e^y)\,\mathbf j+(x+z)\,\mathbf k\) and \(C\) be the curve given by

\[\mathbf r(t)=(1+\sin(20t))\cos t\,\mathbf i+(1+\sin(20t))\sin t\,\mathbf j+\cos(20t)\,\mathbf k\]

with \(0\le t\le2\pi\). Find the line integral \(\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r\).

First check that \(C\) is closed: \(\mathbf r(0)=\langle1,0,1\rangle\) and, since \(\sin40\pi=0\),

\[\mathbf r(2\pi)=\langle\cos2\pi,\ \sin2\pi,\ \cos40\pi\rangle=\langle1,0,1\rangle.\]

Now split \(\mathbf F\) into a gradient field plus a remainder:

\[\begin{aligned}\mathbf F&=\nabla f+x\,\mathbf k,\\ f(x,y,z)&=\frac{x^2}{2}+\cos x+\frac{y^3}{3}+e^y+\frac{z^2}{2}.\end{aligned}\]

Indeed \(f_x=x-\sin x\), \(f_y=y^2+e^y\), \(f_z=z\), and what is left of the \(\mathbf k\)-component is \(x\). (\(\mathbf F\) itself is not conservative: \(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F=\langle0,-1,0\rangle\neq\mathbf 0\).)

By the Fundamental Theorem for Line Integrals, \(\displaystyle\oint_C\nabla f\cdot d\mathbf r=f(\mathbf r(2\pi))-f(\mathbf r(0))=0\) on the closed curve. For the remaining term substitute \(x=(1+\sin20t)\cos t\) and \(dz=-20\sin20t\,dt\):

\[\begin{aligned}\oint_C x\,dz&=\int_0^{2\pi}(1+\sin20t)\cos t\,(-20\sin20t)\,dt\\&=-20\int_0^{2\pi}\cos t\,\sin20t\,dt\\&\quad-20\int_0^{2\pi}\cos t\,\sin^2 20t\,dt.\end{aligned}\]

Product-to-sum formulas give

\[\begin{aligned}\cos t\,\sin20t&=\tfrac12(\sin21t+\sin19t),\\ \cos t\,\sin^2 20t&=\tfrac12\cos t-\tfrac14(\cos41t+\cos39t)\end{aligned}\]

(using \(\sin^2 20t=\tfrac12(1-\cos40t)\)). Since \(\int_0^{2\pi}\sin nt\,dt=\int_0^{2\pi}\cos nt\,dt=0\) for every positive integer \(n\), both integrals vanish. Hence

\[\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\oint_C\nabla f\cdot d\mathbf r+\oint_C x\,dz=0+0=0.\]

Answer. \(0\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • Compute \(\mathbf r(0)=\mathbf r(2\pi)\) to show that \(C\) is a closed curve.
  • \(\mathbf F\) is not conservative (\(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\neq\mathbf 0\)), so "closed curve, hence \(0\)" is not a valid reason. Separate the conservative part \(\nabla f\) from the leftover \(x\,\mathbf k\).
  • Show \(\oint_C x\,dz=0\) with product-to-sum formulas (orthogonality of sines and cosines). Parametrizing everything directly is very long.
22 기말① 3번 세 반원을 따라가는 선적분A line integral along three semicircles10 pts §16.3§16.4
한국어 · 번역

\(\mathbf F(x,y)=\dfrac{-y\,\mathbf i+x\,\mathbf j}{x^2+y^2}\)라 하자. \(C\)가 그림과 같이 \((3,0)\)에서 \((-3,0)\)까지 세 반원을 이은 곡선일 때 선적분 \(\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r\)를 계산하라.

\(P=\dfrac{-y}{x^2+y^2},\ Q=\dfrac{x}{x^2+y^2}\)라 하면 원점이 아닌 점에서 \(\dfrac{\partial Q}{\partial x}=\dfrac{y^2-x^2}{(x^2+y^2)^2}=\dfrac{\partial P}{\partial y}\)이다. 그러나 정의역 \(\mathbb R^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}\)은 단순연결이 아니고, 실제로 \(\mathbf F\)는 거기서 보존적이 아니다(단위원을 한 바퀴 돌면 \(2\pi\)). 그래서 \(C\)를 세 반원 \(C_1,C_2,C_3\)으로 나누어 계산한다.

\(C_1\), \(C_3\): 퍼텐셜 함수 이용. \(x\neq0\)에서 \(f(x,y)=\tan^{-1}\dfrac yx\)라 하면

\[\begin{aligned}\nabla f&=\Bigl\langle\frac{-y/x^2}{1+y^2/x^2},\ \frac{1/x}{1+y^2/x^2}\Bigr\rangle\\&=\Bigl\langle\frac{-y}{x^2+y^2},\ \frac{x}{x^2+y^2}\Bigr\rangle=\mathbf F\end{aligned}\]

이다. \(C_1\)(중심 \((2,0)\), 반지름 \(1\)인 위쪽 반원, \((3,0)\to(1,0)\))은 \(1\le x\le3\)이므로 반평면 \(x>0\)에, \(C_3\)(중심 \((-2,0)\)인 위쪽 반원, \((-1,0)\to(-3,0)\))은 \(-3\le x\le-1\)이므로 반평면 \(x\lt0\)에 들어 있다. 각 반평면에서 \(f\)는 매끄러우므로 선적분의 기본정리에 의해

\[\begin{aligned}\int_{C_1}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r&=f(1,0)-f(3,0)=0-0=0,\\ \int_{C_3}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r&=f(-3,0)-f(-1,0)=0-0=0.\end{aligned}\]

\(C_2\): 직접 계산. \(C_2\)는 원점이 중심인 단위원의 아래쪽 반으로 \((1,0)\to(0,-1)\to(-1,0)\)으로 간다. \(\mathbf r(t)=\langle\cos t,\,-\sin t\rangle\), \(0\le t\le\pi\)로 놓으면 \(x^2+y^2=1\), \(\mathbf r'(t)=\langle-\sin t,\,-\cos t\rangle\)이므로

\[\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))\cdot\mathbf r'(t)=\langle\sin t,\ \cos t\rangle\cdot\langle-\sin t,\ -\cos t\rangle=-1,\]

\(\displaystyle\int_{C_2}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\int_0^\pi(-1)\,dt=-\pi\)이다. 따라서

\[\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=0+(-\pi)+0=-\pi.\]

(검산: \(\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=d\theta\)이므로 적분값은 극각 \(\theta\)의 총 변화량이다. \(C_1\), \(C_3\)에서는 극각이 처음과 끝에서 같고, \(C_2\)에서는 원점을 시계 방향으로 반 바퀴 돌아 \(\theta\)가 \(0\)에서 \(-\pi\)로 변한다.)

답. \(-\pi\).

채점 포인트 · 자주 하는 실수

  • \(\partial Q/\partial x=\partial P/\partial y\)라는 이유만으로 \(\mathbf F\)를 보존적이라 보고 "끝점의 값 차이 \(=0\)"이라 쓰면 틀린다. \(\mathbf F\)는 원점에서 정의되지 않고, \(C_2\)가 원점 주위를 돈다.
  • \(\tan^{-1}(y/x)\)는 반평면 \(x>0\) 또는 \(x\lt0\)에서만 퍼텐셜 함수이다. 각 반원이 어느 반평면에 있는지 밝힌다.
  • \(C_2\)는 시계 방향이므로 매개화 \(\langle\cos t,-\sin t\rangle\)의 향을 확인한다. 반시계로 잡으면 부호가 바뀐다.
English · 원문

Let \(\mathbf F(x,y)=\dfrac{-y\,\mathbf i+x\,\mathbf j}{x^2+y^2}\). Compute the line integral \(\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r\), where \(C\) is the union of three semicircles from \((3,0)\) to \((-3,0)\) as following.

Let \(P=\dfrac{-y}{x^2+y^2},\ Q=\dfrac{x}{x^2+y^2}\). Away from the origin, \(\dfrac{\partial Q}{\partial x}=\dfrac{y^2-x^2}{(x^2+y^2)^2}=\dfrac{\partial P}{\partial y}\). But the domain \(\mathbb R^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}\) is not simply-connected, and in fact \(\mathbf F\) is not conservative there (once around the unit circle gives \(2\pi\)). So split \(C\) into the three semicircles \(C_1,C_2,C_3\).

\(C_1\), \(C_3\): use a potential function. For \(x\neq0\) let \(f(x,y)=\tan^{-1}\dfrac yx\). Then

\[\begin{aligned}\nabla f&=\Bigl\langle\frac{-y/x^2}{1+y^2/x^2},\ \frac{1/x}{1+y^2/x^2}\Bigr\rangle\\&=\Bigl\langle\frac{-y}{x^2+y^2},\ \frac{x}{x^2+y^2}\Bigr\rangle=\mathbf F.\end{aligned}\]

\(C_1\) (upper semicircle, center \((2,0)\), radius \(1\), from \((3,0)\) to \((1,0)\)) has \(1\le x\le3\), so it lies in the half-plane \(x>0\); \(C_3\) (upper semicircle, center \((-2,0)\), from \((-1,0)\) to \((-3,0)\)) has \(-3\le x\le-1\), so it lies in \(x\lt0\). \(f\) is smooth on each half-plane, so by the Fundamental Theorem for Line Integrals

\[\begin{aligned}\int_{C_1}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r&=f(1,0)-f(3,0)=0-0=0,\\ \int_{C_3}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r&=f(-3,0)-f(-1,0)=0-0=0.\end{aligned}\]

\(C_2\): direct computation. \(C_2\) is the lower half of the unit circle centered at the origin, traversed \((1,0)\to(0,-1)\to(-1,0)\). With \(\mathbf r(t)=\langle\cos t,\,-\sin t\rangle\), \(0\le t\le\pi\), we have \(x^2+y^2=1\) and \(\mathbf r'(t)=\langle-\sin t,\,-\cos t\rangle\), so

\[\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))\cdot\mathbf r'(t)=\langle\sin t,\ \cos t\rangle\cdot\langle-\sin t,\ -\cos t\rangle=-1,\]

and \(\displaystyle\int_{C_2}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\int_0^\pi(-1)\,dt=-\pi\). Therefore

\[\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=0+(-\pi)+0=-\pi.\]

(Check: \(\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=d\theta\), so the integral is the net change of the polar angle \(\theta\). On \(C_1\) and \(C_3\) the polar angle ends where it started; on \(C_2\) the curve goes clockwise halfway around the origin, so \(\theta\) goes from \(0\) to \(-\pi\).)

Answer. \(-\pi\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • Concluding from \(\partial Q/\partial x=\partial P/\partial y\) alone that \(\mathbf F\) is conservative, and answering "difference of endpoint values \(=0\)", is wrong: \(\mathbf F\) is undefined at the origin and \(C_2\) goes around it.
  • \(\tan^{-1}(y/x)\) is a potential only on the half-planes \(x>0\) or \(x\lt0\); say which half-plane each semicircle lies in.

\(x^3\) 항. 대칭 \((x,y,z)\mapsto(-x,y,z)\)는 \(S\)를 자기 자신으로 보내고 넓이를 보존하며, \(x^3\)은 \(x\)에 관해 홀함수이므로 \(\displaystyle\iint_S x^3\,dS=0\)이다.

\(z^2\) 항. 구면좌표 매개화 \(\mathbf r(\phi,\theta)=\langle2\sin\phi\cos\theta,\ 2\sin\phi\sin\theta,\ 2\cos\phi\rangle\)에서 \(|\mathbf r_\phi\times\mathbf r_\theta|=4\sin\phi\)이다. 또

\[\begin{aligned}-1\le2\cos\phi\le\sqrt3&\iff-\tfrac12\le\cos\phi\le\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\\&\iff\tfrac\pi6\le\phi\le\tfrac{2\pi}3\end{aligned}\]

이므로

\[\begin{aligned}\iint_S z^2\,dS&=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_{\pi/6}^{2\pi/3}4\cos^2\phi\cdot4\sin\phi\,d\phi\,d\theta\\&=32\pi\Bigl[-\frac{\cos^3\phi}{3}\Bigr]_{\pi/6}^{2\pi/3}\\&=\frac{32\pi}{3}\Bigl(\frac{3\sqrt3}{8}+\frac18\Bigr)=\frac{4\pi(3\sqrt3+1)}{3}.\end{aligned}\]

(참고: 반지름 \(a\)인 구면에서 \(dS=a\,dz\,d\theta\)이므로 \(\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_{-1}^{\sqrt3}z^2\cdot2\,dz\,d\theta\)로 계산해도 같은 값이 나온다.)

답. \(\dfrac{4\pi(3\sqrt3+1)}{3}\approx25.95\).

(2)

원기둥 \(x^2+y^2=2y\)는 \(x^2+(y-1)^2=1\)이고, 극좌표로 \(r=2\sin\theta\) (\(0\le\theta\le\pi\))이다. 원기둥 안에 있는 구면은 위쪽 반구와 아래쪽 반구에 하나씩 있고, 두 조각은 \(xy\)-평면에 대해 대칭이다. 위쪽 조각은 \(z=\sqrt{4-x^2-y^2}\), \((x,y)\in D=\{0\le\theta\le\pi,\ 0\le r\le2\sin\theta\}\)이다(그림).

\(z_x=-\dfrac xz,\ z_y=-\dfrac yz\)이므로

\[\sqrt{1+z_x^2+z_y^2}=\sqrt{\frac{z^2+x^2+y^2}{z^2}}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{4-r^2}}.\]

위쪽 조각의 넓이 \(A_+\)는

\[\begin{aligned}A_+&=\int_0^{\pi}\!\int_0^{2\sin\theta}\frac{2r}{\sqrt{4-r^2}}\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{\pi}\Bigl[-2\sqrt{4-r^2}\Bigr]_0^{2\sin\theta}d\theta\\&=\int_0^{\pi}\bigl(4-2\sqrt{4\cos^2\theta}\bigr)\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{\pi}\bigl(4-4|\cos\theta|\bigr)\,d\theta=4\pi-8.\end{aligned}\]

(\(\sqrt{4\cos^2\theta}=2|\cos\theta|\)이고 \(\int_0^\pi|\cos\theta|\,d\theta=2\)이다. 피적분함수는 점 \((0,2)\) 근처에서만 유계가 아니지만 반복적분은 수렴한다.) 아래쪽 조각의 넓이도 대칭에 의해 \(4\pi-8\)이므로

\[A=2(4\pi-8)=8\pi-16\approx9.13.\]

답. \(8\pi-16\approx9.13\).

(1) Evaluate \(\displaystyle\iint_S x^3+z^2\,dS\), where \(S\) is the surface \(x^2+y^2+z^2=4\), \(-1\le z\le\sqrt3\).

(2) Find the area of the part of the sphere \(x^2+y^2+z^2=4\) that lies inside the cylinder \(x^2+y^2=2y\).

(1)

\(S\) is the zone of the sphere of radius \(2\) with \(-1\le z\le\sqrt3\). Split the integrand into two terms.

The \(x^3\) term. The reflection \((x,y,z)\mapsto(-x,y,z)\) maps \(S\) onto itself and preserves area, while \(x^3\) is odd in \(x\); hence \(\displaystyle\iint_S x^3\,dS=0\).

The \(z^2\) term. With the spherical parametrization \(\mathbf r(\phi,\theta)=\langle2\sin\phi\cos\theta,\ 2\sin\phi\sin\theta,\ 2\cos\phi\rangle\) we have \(|\mathbf r_\phi\times\mathbf r_\theta|=4\sin\phi\). Also

\[\begin{aligned}-1\le2\cos\phi\le\sqrt3&\iff-\tfrac12\le\cos\phi\le\tfrac{\sqrt3}2\\&\iff\tfrac\pi6\le\phi\le\tfrac{2\pi}3,\end{aligned}\]

so

\[\begin{aligned}\iint_S z^2\,dS&=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_{\pi/6}^{2\pi/3}4\cos^2\phi\cdot4\sin\phi\,d\phi\,d\theta\\&=32\pi\Bigl[-\frac{\cos^3\phi}{3}\Bigr]_{\pi/6}^{2\pi/3}\\&=\frac{32\pi}{3}\Bigl(\frac{3\sqrt3}{8}+\frac18\Bigr)=\frac{4\pi(3\sqrt3+1)}{3}.\end{aligned}\]

(Remark: on a sphere of radius \(a\), \(dS=a\,dz\,d\theta\), so \(\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_{-1}^{\sqrt3}z^2\cdot2\,dz\,d\theta\) gives the same value.)

Answer. \(\dfrac{4\pi(3\sqrt3+1)}{3}\approx25.95\).

(2)

The cylinder \(x^2+y^2=2y\) is \(x^2+(y-1)^2=1\), or \(r=2\sin\theta\) (\(0\le\theta\le\pi\)) in polar coordinates. The part of the sphere inside it consists of two pieces, one on the upper hemisphere and one on the lower, symmetric about the \(xy\)-plane. The upper piece is \(z=\sqrt{4-x^2-y^2}\) over \(D=\{0\le\theta\le\pi,\ 0\le r\le2\sin\theta\}\) (figure).

Since \(z_x=-\dfrac xz,\ z_y=-\dfrac yz\),

\[\sqrt{1+z_x^2+z_y^2}=\sqrt{\frac{z^2+x^2+y^2}{z^2}}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{4-r^2}}.\]

The area \(A_+\) of the upper piece is

\[\begin{aligned}A_+&=\int_0^{\pi}\!\int_0^{2\sin\theta}\frac{2r}{\sqrt{4-r^2}}\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{\pi}\Bigl[-2\sqrt{4-r^2}\Bigr]_0^{2\sin\theta}d\theta\\&=\int_0^{\pi}\bigl(4-2\sqrt{4\cos^2\theta}\bigr)\,d\theta\\&=\int_0^{\pi}\bigl(4-4|\cos\theta|\bigr)\,d\theta=4\pi-8.\end{aligned}\]

(\(\sqrt{4\cos^2\theta}=2|\cos\theta|\) and \(\int_0^\pi|\cos\theta|\,d\theta=2\). The integrand is unbounded only near the point \((0,2)\), and the iterated integral converges.) By symmetry the lower piece also has area \(4\pi-8\), so

\[A=2(4\pi-8)=8\pi-16\approx9.13.\]

Answer. \(8\pi-16\approx9.13\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • (1) Say why the \(x^3\) term vanishes (the surface is symmetric about the \(yz\)-plane and \(x^3\) is odd). The range \(\frac\pi6\le\phi\le\frac{2\pi}3\) comes from \(z=2\cos\phi\).
  • (1) On a sphere of radius \(a\), \(dS=a^2\sin\phi\,d\phi\,d\theta\); here \(4\sin\phi\,d\phi\,d\theta\).
  • (2) The cylinder cuts out two pieces of the sphere, upper and lower. Computing only one gives half the answer (\(4\pi-8\)).
  • (2) \(\sqrt{4-4\sin^2\theta}=2|\cos\theta|\): dropping the absolute value makes \(\int_0^\pi\cos\theta\,d\theta=0\) and gives the wrong answer \(8\pi\).

\(C\)가 제1팔분공간에 있는 타원면 \(x^2+\dfrac{y^2}{4}+z^2=1\) 부분의 경계이고 위에서 볼 때 반시계 방향으로 향할 때, 다음 \(\mathbf F\)에 대하여 \(\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r\)를 계산하라.

\[\mathbf F=(yz+2xz)\,\mathbf i+\left(yz+\frac{z^2}{2}\right)\mathbf j+(yx+yz)\,\mathbf k\]

\(P=yz+2xz,\ Q=yz+\frac{z^2}{2},\ R=xy+yz\)이므로

\[\begin{aligned}\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F&=\langle R_y-Q_z,\ P_z-R_x,\ Q_x-P_y\rangle\\&=\langle(x+z)-(y+z),\ (y+2x)-y,\ 0-z\rangle\\&=\langle x-y,\ 2x,\ -z\rangle.\end{aligned}\]

타원면 조각 \(S\)의 경계 \(C\)는 세 좌표평면 위의 사분의 일 타원호 \(C_1\) (\(z=0\)), \(C_2\) (\(x=0\)), \(C_3\) (\(y=0\))로 이루어진다. 위에서 볼 때 반시계 방향이므로 \(C\)는 \((1,0,0)\to(0,2,0)\to(0,0,1)\to(1,0,0)\) 순서로 돌고, 이는 \(S\)의 위쪽(바깥쪽) 법선과 맞는 향이다.

풀이 1: 스토크스 정리. 스토크스 정리(Stokes' Theorem)에 의해 \(\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S\)이다. 이 면적분을 평평한 면으로 옮긴다. 입체 \(E=\{x^2+\frac{y^2}4+z^2\le1,\ x,y,z\ge0\}\)의 경계는 \(S\)와 세 평평한 면 \(S_x\) (\(x=0\)), \(S_y\) (\(y=0\)), \(S_z\) (\(z=0\))이다. \(\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F)=0\)이므로 발산정리에 의해 \(\partial E\) 전체를 지나는 바깥쪽 플럭스는 \(0\)이다. 평평한 면의 바깥쪽 법선은 \(-\mathbf i,-\mathbf j,-\mathbf k\)이므로, \(S\)의 플럭스는 세 면에서 법선을 \(+\mathbf i,+\mathbf j,+\mathbf k\)로 잡은 플럭스의 합과 같다(각 면에서 \(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\)의 \(x,y,z\)성분을 적분).

\[\begin{aligned}\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S&=\iint_{S_x}(x-y)\,dA+\iint_{S_y}2x\,dA\\&\quad+\iint_{S_z}(-z)\,dA.\end{aligned}\]
  • \(S_z\): \(z=0\)이므로 피적분함수가 \(0\)이다.
  • \(S_x\): \(x=0\)이므로 피적분함수는 \(-y\), 영역은 \(\frac{y^2}4+z^2\le1,\ y,z\ge0\)이다. \[\begin{aligned}\iint_{S_x}(-y)\,dA&=-\int_0^2 y\sqrt{1-\tfrac{y^2}{4}}\,dy\\&=\Bigl[\tfrac43\bigl(1-\tfrac{y^2}4\bigr)^{3/2}\Bigr]_0^2=-\frac43.\end{aligned}\]
  • \(S_y\): \(y=0\)이므로 피적분함수는 \(2x\), 영역은 사분원 \(x^2+z^2\le1,\ x,z\ge0\)이다. \(x=r\cos\theta,\ z=r\sin\theta\)로 놓으면 \[\iint_{S_y}2x\,dA=\int_0^{\pi/2}\!\int_0^1 2r\cos\theta\,r\,dr\,d\theta=\frac23.\]

따라서 \(\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=0-\frac43+\frac23=-\frac23\)이다.

풀이 2: 직접 계산(검산). 각 호를 \(0\le t\le\frac\pi2\)로 매개화한다.

  • \(C_1\): \(x=\cos t,\ y=2\sin t,\ z=0\). \(P=Q=0\)이고 \(dz=0\)이므로 \(\int_{C_1}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=0\).
  • \(C_2\): \(x=0,\ y=2\cos t,\ z=\sin t\). \(dx=0\)이고 \[\begin{aligned}Q\,dy+R\,dz&=\bigl(2\sin t\cos t+\tfrac12\sin^2t\bigr)(-2\sin t)\,dt\\&\quad+(2\cos t\sin t)\cos t\,dt\\&=\bigl(-4\sin^2t\cos t-\sin^3t+2\sin t\cos^2t\bigr)\,dt\end{aligned}\] 이므로 \(\int_{C_2}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=-\frac43-\frac23+\frac23=-\frac43\).
  • \(C_3\): \(x=\sin t,\ y=0,\ z=\cos t\). \(Q\,dy=0\), \(R=0\)이고 \(P\,dx=2\sin t\cos t\cdot\cos t\,dt\)이므로 \(\int_{C_3}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\frac23\).

합은 \(0-\frac43+\frac23=-\frac23\)으로 풀이 1과 같다.

답. \(-\dfrac23\).

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r\), where

\[\mathbf F=(yz+2xz)\,\mathbf i+\left(yz+\frac{z^2}{2}\right)\mathbf j+(yx+yz)\,\mathbf k\]

and \(C\) is the boundary of the part of the ellipsoid \(x^2+\dfrac{y^2}{4}+z^2=1\) in the first octant, oriented counterclockwise as viewed from above.

With \(P=yz+2xz,\ Q=yz+\frac{z^2}{2},\ R=xy+yz\),

\[\begin{aligned}\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F&=\langle R_y-Q_z,\ P_z-R_x,\ Q_x-P_y\rangle\\&=\langle(x+z)-(y+z),\ (y+2x)-y,\ 0-z\rangle\\&=\langle x-y,\ 2x,\ -z\rangle.\end{aligned}\]

The boundary \(C\) of the ellipsoid piece \(S\) consists of three quarter-ellipse arcs in the coordinate planes: \(C_1\) (\(z=0\)), \(C_2\) (\(x=0\)), \(C_3\) (\(y=0\)). Counterclockwise as viewed from above means \(C\) runs \((1,0,0)\to(0,2,0)\to(0,0,1)\to(1,0,0)\), which matches the upward (outward) normal of \(S\).

Method 1: Stokes' Theorem. By Stokes' Theorem, \(\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S\). Move this surface integral onto flat faces. The solid \(E=\{x^2+\frac{y^2}4+z^2\le1,\ x,y,z\ge0\}\) is bounded by \(S\) and the three flat faces \(S_x\) (\(x=0\)), \(S_y\) (\(y=0\)), \(S_z\) (\(z=0\)). Since \(\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F)=0\), the Divergence Theorem says the outward flux of \(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\) through all of \(\partial E\) is \(0\). The outward normals of the flat faces are \(-\mathbf i,-\mathbf j,-\mathbf k\), so the flux through \(S\) equals the sum of the fluxes through the three faces taken with normals \(+\mathbf i,+\mathbf j,+\mathbf k\) (integrate the \(x\)-, \(y\)-, \(z\)-component of \(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\) on the respective face):

\[\begin{aligned}\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S&=\iint_{S_x}(x-y)\,dA+\iint_{S_y}2x\,dA\\&\quad+\iint_{S_z}(-z)\,dA.\end{aligned}\]
  • \(S_z\): \(z=0\), so the integrand is \(0\).
  • \(S_x\): \(x=0\), so the integrand is \(-y\) over \(\frac{y^2}4+z^2\le1,\ y,z\ge0\): \[\begin{aligned}\iint_{S_x}(-y)\,dA&=-\int_0^2 y\sqrt{1-\tfrac{y^2}{4}}\,dy\\&=\Bigl[\tfrac43\bigl(1-\tfrac{y^2}4\bigr)^{3/2}\Bigr]_0^2=-\frac43.\end{aligned}\]
  • \(S_y\): \(y=0\), so the integrand is \(2x\) over the quarter disk \(x^2+z^2\le1,\ x,z\ge0\). With \(x=r\cos\theta,\ z=r\sin\theta\), \[\iint_{S_y}2x\,dA=\int_0^{\pi/2}\!\int_0^1 2r\cos\theta\,r\,dr\,d\theta=\frac23.\]

Hence \(\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=0-\frac43+\frac23=-\frac23\).

Method 2: direct computation (check). Parametrize each arc with \(0\le t\le\frac\pi2\).

  • \(C_1\): \(x=\cos t,\ y=2\sin t,\ z=0\). Here \(P=Q=0\) and \(dz=0\), so \(\int_{C_1}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=0\).
  • \(C_2\): \(x=0,\ y=2\cos t,\ z=\sin t\). Then \(dx=0\) and \[\begin{aligned}Q\,dy+R\,dz&=\bigl(2\sin t\cos t+\tfrac12\sin^2t\bigr)(-2\sin t)\,dt\\&\quad+(2\cos t\sin t)\cos t\,dt\\&=\bigl(-4\sin^2t\cos t-\sin^3t+2\sin t\cos^2t\bigr)\,dt,\end{aligned}\] so \(\int_{C_2}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=-\frac43-\frac23+\frac23=-\frac43\).
  • \(C_3\): \(x=\sin t,\ y=0,\ z=\cos t\). Here \(Q\,dy=0\), \(R=0\) and \(P\,dx=2\sin t\cos t\cdot\cos t\,dt\), so \(\int_{C_3}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\frac23\).

The sum is \(0-\frac43+\frac23=-\frac23\), as in Method 1.

Answer. \(-\dfrac23\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • Get \(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F=\langle x-y,\ 2x,\ -z\rangle\) right (\(R_y=x+z\), \(Q_z=y+z\)).
  • State that \(C\) is three arcs in the coordinate planes and that "counterclockwise from above" means the order \((1,0,0)\to(0,2,0)\to(0,0,1)\).
  • The surface integral over the curved ellipsoid is messy. Either move it to the three flat faces using \(\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F)=0\), or compute the line integral directly. When switching surfaces, watch the normal signs (\(+\mathbf i,+\mathbf j,+\mathbf k\)).

평면 \(x-2z=3\) 중 원기둥 \(y^2+z^2=1\)의 내부에 있고 \(x\)축의 양의 방향으로 향한 곡면 \(S\)와 다음 벡터장에 대하여 스토크스 정리가 성립함을 확인하라.

\[\mathbf F(x,y,z)=2z\,\mathbf i+xy\,\mathbf j+(z+2y)\,\mathbf k\]

\(P=2z,\ Q=xy,\ R=z+2y\)이므로

\[\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F=\langle R_y-Q_z,\ P_z-R_x,\ Q_x-P_y\rangle=\langle2,\ 2,\ y\rangle.\]

면적분. \(S\)는 평면 \(x=3+2z\) 중 \(y^2+z^2\le1\)인 부분이므로 \(\mathbf r(y,z)=\langle3+2z,\ y,\ z\rangle\), \((y,z)\in D=\{y^2+z^2\le1\}\)로 매개화한다.

\[\mathbf r_y\times\mathbf r_z=\langle0,1,0\rangle\times\langle2,0,1\rangle=\langle1,\ 0,\ -2\rangle\]

의 \(x\)성분이 양수이므로 이 법선이 문제의 향(양의 \(x\)축 방향)과 맞는다.

\[\begin{aligned}\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S&=\iint_D\langle2,2,y\rangle\cdot\langle1,0,-2\rangle\,dA\\&=\iint_D(2-2y)\,dA=2\pi-0=2\pi.\end{aligned}\]

(\(D\)의 넓이는 \(\pi\)이고, \(D\)가 \(y\to-y\)에 대해 대칭이므로 \(\iint_D y\,dA=0\).)

선적분. 경계 \(C\)는 원기둥 \(y^2+z^2=1\)과 평면 \(x=3+2z\)의 교선(타원)이다. 양의 \(x\)축 쪽에서 보면 \(y\)축은 오른쪽, \(z\)축은 위쪽이므로, \(S\)의 향과 맞는 \(C\)의 향은 \((y,z)\)가 반시계로 도는 방향이다(매개변수 영역 \(D\)의 양의 향 경계의 상).

\[\mathbf r(t)=\langle3+2\sin t,\ \cos t,\ \sin t\rangle,\quad0\le t\le2\pi.\]

\(\mathbf r'(t)=\langle2\cos t,\ -\sin t,\ \cos t\rangle\), \(\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))=\langle2\sin t,\ (3+2\sin t)\cos t,\ \sin t+2\cos t\rangle\)이므로

\[\begin{aligned}\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf r'&=4\sin t\cos t-(3+2\sin t)\sin t\cos t\\&\quad+(\sin t+2\cos t)\cos t\\&=2\sin t\cos t-2\sin^2t\cos t+2\cos^2t.\end{aligned}\] \[\begin{aligned}\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r&=\int_0^{2\pi}\bigl(\sin2t-2\sin^2t\cos t+2\cos^2t\bigr)\,dt\\&=0-\Bigl[\tfrac23\sin^3t\Bigr]_0^{2\pi}+2\pi=2\pi.\end{aligned}\]

두 값이 모두 \(2\pi\)이므로 스토크스 정리 \(\displaystyle\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S\)가 성립함을 확인했다.

답. \(\displaystyle\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=2\pi\).

English · 원문

Verify that Stokes' Theorem is true for the given vector field

\[\mathbf F(x,y,z)=2z\,\mathbf i+xy\,\mathbf j+(z+2y)\,\mathbf k\]

and the surface \(S\) which is the part of the plane \(x-2z=3\) that lies inside of the cylinder \(y^2+z^2=1\), oriented in the direction of positive \(x\)-axis.

With \(P=2z,\ Q=xy,\ R=z+2y\),

\[\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F=\langle R_y-Q_z,\ P_z-R_x,\ Q_x-P_y\rangle=\langle2,\ 2,\ y\rangle.\]

Surface integral. \(S\) is the part of the plane \(x=3+2z\) with \(y^2+z^2\le1\), so parametrize it by \(\mathbf r(y,z)=\langle3+2z,\ y,\ z\rangle\), \((y,z)\in D=\{y^2+z^2\le1\}\).

\[\mathbf r_y\times\mathbf r_z=\langle0,1,0\rangle\times\langle2,0,1\rangle=\langle1,\ 0,\ -2\rangle\]

has positive \(x\)-component, so this normal matches the given orientation (positive \(x\)-direction).

\[\begin{aligned}\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S&=\iint_D\langle2,2,y\rangle\cdot\langle1,0,-2\rangle\,dA\\&=\iint_D(2-2y)\,dA=2\pi-0=2\pi.\end{aligned}\]

(\(D\) has area \(\pi\), and \(D\) is symmetric under \(y\to-y\), so \(\iint_D y\,dA=0\).)

Line integral. The boundary \(C\) is the ellipse where the cylinder \(y^2+z^2=1\) meets the plane \(x=3+2z\). Viewed from the positive \(x\)-axis, the \(y\)-axis points right and the \(z\)-axis points up, so the orientation of \(C\) compatible with \(S\) is the one in which \((y,z)\) goes counterclockwise (the image of the positively oriented boundary of the parameter domain \(D\)):

\[\mathbf r(t)=\langle3+2\sin t,\ \cos t,\ \sin t\rangle,\quad0\le t\le2\pi.\]

Then \(\mathbf r'(t)=\langle2\cos t,\ -\sin t,\ \cos t\rangle\) and \(\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))=\langle2\sin t,\ (3+2\sin t)\cos t,\ \sin t+2\cos t\rangle\), so

\[\begin{aligned}\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf r'&=4\sin t\cos t-(3+2\sin t)\sin t\cos t\\&\quad+(\sin t+2\cos t)\cos t\\&=2\sin t\cos t-2\sin^2t\cos t+2\cos^2t.\end{aligned}\] \[\begin{aligned}\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r&=\int_0^{2\pi}\bigl(\sin2t-2\sin^2t\cos t+2\cos^2t\bigr)\,dt\\&=0-\Bigl[\tfrac23\sin^3t\Bigr]_0^{2\pi}+2\pi=2\pi.\end{aligned}\]

Both sides equal \(2\pi\), which verifies Stokes' Theorem \(\displaystyle\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S\).

Answer. \(\displaystyle\iint_S\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=2\pi\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • "Verify" means computing both sides separately. Computing one side and quoting the theorem does not answer the question.
  • Check that \(\mathbf r_y\times\mathbf r_z=\langle1,0,-2\rangle\) has positive \(x\)-component and that the boundary orientation (\(y=\cos t,\ z=\sin t\)) matches it. Using \(\mathbf r_z\times\mathbf r_y\) flips the sign.
  • On the boundary \(x=3+2\sin t\) varies (do not freeze \(x=3\)). The parameter domain \(D\) is the unit disk.

\(\det A\). 넷째 열에서 첫째 열을 빼도(\(C_4\to C_4-C_1\)) 행렬식은 변하지 않는다. 그 다음 0이 많은 둘째 행으로 여인수 전개한다.

따라서 \(a\neq0\)이면 \(\det A\neq0\)이고 크래머 공식을 쓸 수 있다.

\(\det A_2\). \(A\)의 둘째 열을 \(\mathbf d\)로 바꾼 행렬 \(A_2\)에도 같은 열 연산과 전개를 적용한다.

\[\begin{aligned}\det A_2&=\begin{vmatrix}1&1&1&1\\a&0&0&a\\2&1&c&2-c\\3&0&1&2\end{vmatrix}\\&=\begin{vmatrix}1&1&1&0\\a&0&0&0\\2&1&c&-c\\3&0&1&-1\end{vmatrix}\\&=-a\begin{vmatrix}1&1&0\\1&c&-c\\0&1&-1\end{vmatrix}=-a\bigl[1\cdot0-1\cdot(-1)+0\bigr]=-a.\end{aligned}\]

\[x_2=\frac{\det A_2}{\det A}=\frac{-a}{-a}=1.\]

(검산: 전체 해는 \(x_1=b-1,\ x_2=1,\ x_3=x_4=1-b\)이고, 네 식에 대입하면 모두 성립한다.)

(1)

Let \(A\) be the coefficient matrix, \(\mathbf x\) the vector of unknowns and \(\mathbf d\) the constant vector. The system is \(A\mathbf x=\mathbf d\):

\[\begin{pmatrix}1&b&1&1\\a&0&0&a\\2&1&c&2-c\\3&0&1&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\x_2\\x_3\\x_4\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}\]

\(\det A\). Subtracting column 1 from column 4 (\(C_4\to C_4-C_1\)) does not change the determinant. Then expand along the second row, which is mostly zeros:

\[\begin{aligned}\det A&=\begin{vmatrix}1&b&1&0\\a&0&0&0\\2&1&c&-c\\3&0&1&-1\end{vmatrix}\\&=(-1)^{2+1}a\begin{vmatrix}b&1&0\\1&c&-c\\0&1&-1\end{vmatrix}\\&=-a\bigl[b(-c+c)-1\cdot(-1-0)+0\bigr]=-a.\end{aligned}\]

So for \(a\neq0\), \(\det A\neq0\) and Cramer's rule applies.

\(\det A_2\). Replace the second column of \(A\) by \(\mathbf d\) to get \(A_2\), and apply the same column operation and expansion:

\[\begin{aligned}\det A_2&=\begin{vmatrix}1&1&1&1\\a&0&0&a\\2&1&c&2-c\\3&0&1&2\end{vmatrix}\\&=\begin{vmatrix}1&1&1&0\\a&0&0&0\\2&1&c&-c\\3&0&1&-1\end{vmatrix}\\&=-a\begin{vmatrix}1&1&0\\1&c&-c\\0&1&-1\end{vmatrix}=-a\bigl[1\cdot0-1\cdot(-1)+0\bigr]=-a.\end{aligned}\]

By Cramer's rule,

\[x_2=\frac{\det A_2}{\det A}=\frac{-a}{-a}=1.\]

(Check: the full solution is \(x_1=b-1,\ x_2=1,\ x_3=x_4=1-b\), which satisfies all four equations.)

Answer. \(A\mathbf x=\mathbf d\) (matrices above), \(\det A=-a\); for \(a\neq0\), \(x_2=1\).

(2)

a. For \(3\times3\) matrices, \(\det(cM)=c^3\det M\) and \(\det(XY)=\det X\,\det Y\). The first factor is upper triangular, so its determinant is the product of the diagonal entries, \(1\cdot1\cdot(-2)=-2\). For the second, expand along the first row:

\[\begin{vmatrix}0&0&1\\0&2&3\\4&5&6\end{vmatrix}=(+1)\cdot1\cdot\begin{vmatrix}0&2\\4&5\end{vmatrix}=0\cdot5-2\cdot4=-8.\]

Hence \(\det B=2^3\cdot(-2)\cdot(-8)\cdot k=128k\).

b. Factor \(2\) out of column 1, then \(3\) out of row 1 and \(-2\) out of row 2:

\[\begin{aligned}\det C&=2\begin{vmatrix}3x&3y&3z\\-2\alpha&-2\beta&-2\gamma\\a&b&c\end{vmatrix}\\&=2\cdot3\cdot(-2)\begin{vmatrix}x&y&z\\\alpha&\beta&\gamma\\a&b&c\end{vmatrix}=-12\cdot(-k)=12k.\end{aligned}\]

(The last matrix is \(A\) with rows 1 and 3 swapped, so its determinant is \(-k\).)

c. Column 1 is \(\langle\alpha,\beta,\gamma\rangle+\langle x,y,z\rangle\), so split by linearity in column 1:

\[\begin{aligned}\det D&=\begin{vmatrix}\alpha&a&2x\\\beta&b&2y\\\gamma&c&2z\end{vmatrix}+\begin{vmatrix}x&a&2x\\y&b&2y\\z&c&2z\end{vmatrix}\\&=2\begin{vmatrix}\alpha&a&x\\\beta&b&y\\\gamma&c&z\end{vmatrix}+0.\end{aligned}\]

In the second matrix column 3 is twice column 1, so its determinant is \(0\). Transposing the remaining matrix (\(\det M^T=\det M\)) gives rows \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma),(a,b,c),(x,y,z)\), which is \(A\) with rows 1 and 2 swapped, so its determinant is \(-k\). Therefore \(\det D=2\cdot(-k)=-2k\).

Answer. a. \(\det B=128k\)   b. \(\det C=12k\)   c. \(\det D=-2k\)

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • (1) Write the matrix form \(A\mathbf x=\mathbf d\) first, and say that \(A_2\) is \(A\) with its second column replaced by the constant vector. Cramer's rule needs \(\det A=-a\neq0\).
  • (1) Expanding the \(4\times4\) determinant along the mostly-zero second row (after \(C_4-C_1\)) keeps it short. Watch the cofactor signs \((-1)^{i+j}\).
  • (2) Apply \(\det(cM)=c^3\det M\) (in a, the factor is \(2^3=8\), not \(2\)), common factors of rows and columns, the sign change for a row swap, \(\det M^T=\det M\), and "two proportional columns give \(0\)" correctly.
