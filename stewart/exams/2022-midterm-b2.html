22 중간② 1번 두 변수 함수의 극한Limits of functions of two variables15 pts §14.2
한국어 · 번역

극한이 존재하면 그 값을 구하고, 존재하지 않으면 존재하지 않음을 보여라.

(a) \(\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\frac{4x^3+xy}{x^2+y^2}\)

(b) \(\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\frac{x^2y}{x^2+y^2}\sin\!\left(\frac{1}{x^2+y^2}\right)\)

(a)

\(y=0\)을 따라 \((x,0)\to(0,0)\)이면

\[\frac{4x^3}{x^2}=4x\to0.\]

\(y=x\)를 따라 \((x,x)\to(0,0)\)이면

\[\frac{4x^3+x^2}{2x^2}=2x+\frac12\to\frac12.\]

두 경로에서 극한값이 \(0\neq\frac12\)로 다르므로 극한은 존재하지 않는다.

답. 극한은 존재하지 않는다.

(b)

\((x,y)\neq(0,0)\)에서 \(0\le\dfrac{x^2}{x^2+y^2}\le1\)이고 \(\left|\sin\left(\dfrac{1}{x^2+y^2}\right)\right|\le1\)이므로

\[0\le\left|\frac{x^2y}{x^2+y^2}\sin\!\left(\frac{1}{x^2+y^2}\right)\right|\le\frac{x^2}{x^2+y^2}\,|y|\le|y|.\]

\((x,y)\to(0,0)\)이면 \(|y|\to0\)이므로 조임정리(Squeeze Theorem)에 의해 극한은 \(0\)이다.

답. \(0\).

채점 포인트 · 자주 하는 실수

  • (a) \(y=0\)이나 \(x=0\) 경로는 둘 다 \(0\)을 주므로 이것만으로 "극한 \(0\)"이라 하면 틀린다. 값이 다른 두 경로와 각각의 극한값을 쓴다. (일반적으로 \(y=mx\)이면 \(\frac{m}{1+m^2}\)으로 \(m\)에 의존한다.)
  • (b) \(\sin(1/(x^2+y^2))\) 자체는 극한이 없으므로 "극한의 곱"으로 쪼갤 수 없다. \(|\sin|\le1\)과 \(\frac{x^2}{x^2+y^2}\le1\)로 위에서 눌러 조임정리를 쓴다.
English · 원문

Find the limit, if it exists, or show that the limit does not exist.

(a) \(\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\frac{4x^3+xy}{x^2+y^2}\)

(b) \(\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\frac{x^2y}{x^2+y^2}\sin\!\left(\frac{1}{x^2+y^2}\right)\)

(a)

Along \(y=0\), \((x,0)\to(0,0)\):

\[\frac{4x^3}{x^2}=4x\to0.\]

Along \(y=x\), \((x,x)\to(0,0)\):

\[\frac{4x^3+x^2}{2x^2}=2x+\frac12\to\frac12.\]

The two paths give different limits, \(0\neq\frac12\), so the limit does not exist.

Answer. The limit does not exist.

(b)

For \((x,y)\neq(0,0)\) we have \(0\le\dfrac{x^2}{x^2+y^2}\le1\) and \(\left|\sin\left(\dfrac{1}{x^2+y^2}\right)\right|\le1\), so

\[0\le\left|\frac{x^2y}{x^2+y^2}\sin\!\left(\frac{1}{x^2+y^2}\right)\right|\le\frac{x^2}{x^2+y^2}\,|y|\le|y|.\]

Since \(|y|\to0\) as \((x,y)\to(0,0)\), the Squeeze Theorem gives the limit \(0\).

Answer. \(0\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • (a) The paths \(y=0\) and \(x=0\) both give \(0\), so they alone prove nothing. Give two paths with different limits and state each value. (In general, along \(y=mx\) the limit is \(\frac{m}{1+m^2}\), which depends on \(m\).)
  • (b) \(\sin(1/(x^2+y^2))\) has no limit, so you cannot split the expression into a product of limits. Bound it using \(|\sin|\le1\) and \(\frac{x^2}{x^2+y^2}\le1\), then apply the Squeeze Theorem.
22 중간② 2번 구간별 정의 함수의 연속성과 편도함수Continuity and partial derivatives of a piecewise function15 pts §14.2§14.3
한국어 · 번역

함수 \(f\)가 다음과 같다.

\[f(x,y)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^3+3y^5}{x^2+x^2y^2+y^4}, & (x,y)\neq(0,0)\\[2mm] 0, & (x,y)=(0,0)\end{cases}\]

(a) \(f(x,y)\)가 점 \((0,0)\)에서 연속임을 보여라.

(b) \(f_x(0,0)\)과 \(f_{xx}(0,0)\)을 구하라.

(a)

\((x,y)\neq(0,0)\)이면 분모 \(x^2+x^2y^2+y^4\)는 양수이고, \(x^2y^2\ge0\)이므로 분모 \(\ge x^2\), 분모 \(\ge y^4\)이다. 따라서

\[\begin{aligned}\frac{|x|^3}{x^2+x^2y^2+y^4}&\le\frac{|x|^3}{x^2}=|x|,\\\frac{3|y|^5}{x^2+x^2y^2+y^4}&\le\frac{3|y|^5}{y^4}=3|y|\end{aligned}\]

이다(\(x=0\)이면 왼쪽 항이 \(0\), \(y=0\)이면 오른쪽 항이 \(0\)이므로 부등식은 그대로 성립한다). 삼각부등식에 의해

\[0\le|f(x,y)|\le|x|+3|y|.\]

\((x,y)\to(0,0)\)이면 \(|x|+3|y|\to0\)이므로 조임정리에 의해 \(\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}f(x,y)=0=f(0,0)\)이다. 따라서 \(f\)는 \((0,0)\)에서 연속이다.

답. \(|f(x,y)|\le|x|+3|y|\to0\)이므로 \(\lim f=0=f(0,0)\), 연속이다.

(b)

\(x\neq0\)이면 \(f(x,0)=\dfrac{x^3}{x^2}=x\)이고 \(f(0,0)=0\)이므로 모든 \(x\)에 대해 \(f(x,0)=x\)이다. 원점에서는 공식이 정의되지 않으므로 정의로 계산한다.

\[f_x(0,0)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(h,0)-f(0,0)}{h}=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{h}{h}=1.\]

\(h\neq0\)일 때 \(f_x(h,0)\)은 직선 \(y=0\) 위의 값 \(f(x,0)=x\)만으로 정해지므로 \(f_x(h,0)=1\)이다. (몫의 미분법으로 구한 \(f_x=\dfrac{3x^2(x^2+x^2y^2+y^4)-(x^3+3y^5)(2x+2xy^2)}{(x^2+x^2y^2+y^4)^2}\)에 \(y=0\)을 넣어도 \(\dfrac{3x^4-2x^4}{x^4}=1\)이다.) 따라서

\[f_{xx}(0,0)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f_x(h,0)-f_x(0,0)}{h}=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{1-1}{h}=0.\]

답. \(f_x(0,0)=1\), \(f_{xx}(0,0)=0\).

채점 포인트 · 자주 하는 실수

  • (a) 분모를 \(x^2\)과 \(y^4\)으로 각각 아래에서 눌러 두 항을 따로 평가한다. 경로 몇 개로 확인하는 것은 연속의 증명이 아니다.
  • (b) \((0,0)\)에서는 편도함수의 정의(극한)로 계산한다. 공식을 미분해 \((0,0)\)을 대입하면 \(\frac00\)이 된다.
  • \(f_{xx}(0,0)\)은 \(f_x(h,0)\) (\(h\neq0\))이 필요하다. \(y=0\) 위에서 \(f_x(h,0)=1\)임을 근거와 함께 쓴다.
English · 원문

Let

\[f(x,y)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^3+3y^5}{x^2+x^2y^2+y^4}, & (x,y)\neq(0,0)\\[2mm] 0, & (x,y)=(0,0)\end{cases}.\]

(a) Show that \(f(x,y)\) is continuous at the point \((0,0)\).

(b) Find \(f_x(0,0)\) and \(f_{xx}(0,0)\).

(a)

For \((x,y)\neq(0,0)\) the denominator \(x^2+x^2y^2+y^4\) is positive, and since \(x^2y^2\ge0\) it is at least \(x^2\) and at least \(y^4\). Hence

\[\begin{aligned}\frac{|x|^3}{x^2+x^2y^2+y^4}&\le\frac{|x|^3}{x^2}=|x|,\\\frac{3|y|^5}{x^2+x^2y^2+y^4}&\le\frac{3|y|^5}{y^4}=3|y|\end{aligned}\]

(if \(x=0\) the first term is \(0\), and if \(y=0\) the second is \(0\), so the bounds still hold). By the triangle inequality,

\[0\le|f(x,y)|\le|x|+3|y|.\]

As \((x,y)\to(0,0)\), \(|x|+3|y|\to0\), so by the Squeeze Theorem \(\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}f(x,y)=0=f(0,0)\). Therefore \(f\) is continuous at \((0,0)\).

Answer. \(|f(x,y)|\le|x|+3|y|\to0\), so \(\lim f=0=f(0,0)\): \(f\) is continuous.

(b)

For \(x\neq0\), \(f(x,0)=\dfrac{x^3}{x^2}=x\), and \(f(0,0)=0\), so \(f(x,0)=x\) for every \(x\). The formula is undefined at the origin, so use the definition:

\[f_x(0,0)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(h,0)-f(0,0)}{h}=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{h}{h}=1.\]

For \(h\neq0\), \(f_x(h,0)\) depends only on the values \(f(x,0)=x\) along \(y=0\), so \(f_x(h,0)=1\). (Equivalently, setting \(y=0\) in the quotient-rule formula \(f_x=\dfrac{3x^2(x^2+x^2y^2+y^4)-(x^3+3y^5)(2x+2xy^2)}{(x^2+x^2y^2+y^4)^2}\) gives \(\dfrac{3x^4-2x^4}{x^4}=1\).) Therefore

\[f_{xx}(0,0)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f_x(h,0)-f_x(0,0)}{h}=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{1-1}{h}=0.\]

Answer. \(f_x(0,0)=1\), \(f_{xx}(0,0)=0\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • (a) Bound the denominator below by \(x^2\) and by \(y^4\) separately and estimate the two terms one at a time. Checking a few paths does not prove continuity.
  • (b) At \((0,0)\) use the limit definition of the partial derivative. Differentiating the formula and plugging in \((0,0)\) gives \(\frac00\).
  • \(f_{xx}(0,0)\) needs \(f_x(h,0)\) for \(h\neq0\); justify \(f_x(h,0)=1\) from the values on \(y=0\).
22 중간② 3번 음함수의 최대 증가율과 선형근사Implicit function: fastest increase and linear approximation15 pts §14.6§14.4
한국어 · 번역

\(f(x,y,z)=x^2-2y^3+3z^4-2xyz\)라 하자. \(g(1,-1)=1\)이고 \(f(x,y,g(x,y))=8\)을 만족하는 미분가능한 함수 \(g(x,y)\)가 존재한다고 하자.

(a) \((1,-1)\)에서 \(g(x,y)\)가 가장 빠르게 증가하는 방향으로의 방향도함수를 구하라.

(b) 선형근사를 이용하여 \(g(1.3,-1.2)\)를 추정하라.

먼저 \(f(1,-1,1)=1+2+3+2=8\)이므로 점 \((1,-1,1)\)은 조건과 맞는다. \(f(x,y,g(x,y))=8\)의 양변을 \(x\)와 \(y\)에 대해 편미분하면(연쇄법칙)

\[f_x+f_z\,g_x=0,\qquad f_y+f_z\,g_y=0\ \Longrightarrow\ g_x=-\frac{f_x}{f_z},\quad g_y=-\frac{f_y}{f_z}.\]

\(f_x=2x-2yz,\ f_y=-6y^2-2xz,\ f_z=12z^3-2xy\)이므로 \((1,-1,1)\)에서

\[f_x=2+2=4,\qquad f_y=-6-2=-8,\qquad f_z=12+2=14.\]

따라서 \(g_x(1,-1)=-\dfrac{4}{14}=-\dfrac27\), \(g_y(1,-1)=\dfrac{8}{14}=\dfrac47\), 즉 \(\nabla g(1,-1)=\left\langle-\dfrac27,\dfrac47\right\rangle\).

(a)

방향도함수 \(D_{\mathbf u}g=\nabla g\cdot\mathbf u\)는 \(\mathbf u\)가 \(\nabla g\)와 같은 방향일 때 최대이고, 그 값은 \(|\nabla g|\)이다. 이 방향은 \(\mathbf u=\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\langle-1,2\rangle\)이고

\[|\nabla g(1,-1)|=\frac{\sqrt{4+16}}{7}=\frac{2\sqrt5}{7}.\]

답. \(\dfrac{2\sqrt5}{7}\) (방향 \(\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\langle-1,2\rangle\)).

(b)

\((1,-1)\)에서의 선형화는

\[L(x,y)=1-\frac27(x-1)+\frac47(y+1).\]

\(x-1=0.3,\ y+1=-0.2\)이므로

\[g(1.3,-1.2)\approx1-\frac{0.6}{7}-\frac{0.8}{7}=1-0.2=0.8.\]

답. \(g(1.3,-1.2)\approx0.8\).

채점 포인트 · 자주 하는 실수

  • 음함수 미분 \(g_x=-f_x/f_z,\ g_y=-f_y/f_z\)의 부호와, 편도함수를 점 \((1,-1,1)\) (\(z=g(1,-1)=1\))에서 평가하는 것을 확인한다.
  • (a) 최대 증가 방향의 방향도함수는 \(|\nabla g|\)라는 수이다. 벡터 \(\nabla g\)를 답으로 쓰지 않는다.
  • (b) 증분은 \(\Delta x=0.3,\ \Delta y=-0.2\)이다(\(y\)의 부호 주의).
English · 원문

Let \(f(x,y,z)=x^2-2y^3+3z^4-2xyz\). Suppose that there exists a differentiable function \(g(x,y)\) such that \(g(1,-1)=1\) and \(f(x,y,g(x,y))=8\).

(a) Find the directional derivative of \(g(x,y)\) at \((1,-1)\) in direction of the fastest increasing.

(b) Use the linear approximation to the estimate \(g(1.3,-1.2)\).

First, \(f(1,-1,1)=1+2+3+2=8\), so the point \((1,-1,1)\) is consistent with the data. Differentiating \(f(x,y,g(x,y))=8\) partially with respect to \(x\) and \(y\) (Chain Rule):

\[f_x+f_z\,g_x=0,\qquad f_y+f_z\,g_y=0\ \Longrightarrow\ g_x=-\frac{f_x}{f_z},\quad g_y=-\frac{f_y}{f_z}.\]

With \(f_x=2x-2yz,\ f_y=-6y^2-2xz,\ f_z=12z^3-2xy\), at \((1,-1,1)\):

\[f_x=2+2=4,\qquad f_y=-6-2=-8,\qquad f_z=12+2=14.\]

So \(g_x(1,-1)=-\dfrac{4}{14}=-\dfrac27\) and \(g_y(1,-1)=\dfrac{8}{14}=\dfrac47\), i.e. \(\nabla g(1,-1)=\left\langle-\dfrac27,\dfrac47\right\rangle\).

(a)

The directional derivative \(D_{\mathbf u}g=\nabla g\cdot\mathbf u\) is largest when \(\mathbf u\) points along \(\nabla g\), and the largest value is \(|\nabla g|\). That direction is \(\mathbf u=\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\langle-1,2\rangle\), and

\[|\nabla g(1,-1)|=\frac{\sqrt{4+16}}{7}=\frac{2\sqrt5}{7}.\]

Answer. \(\dfrac{2\sqrt5}{7}\) (direction \(\frac{1}{\sqrt5}\langle-1,2\rangle\)).

(b)

The linearization at \((1,-1)\) is

\[L(x,y)=1-\frac27(x-1)+\frac47(y+1).\]

With \(x-1=0.3,\ y+1=-0.2\),

\[g(1.3,-1.2)\approx1-\frac{0.6}{7}-\frac{0.8}{7}=1-0.2=0.8.\]

Answer. \(g(1.3,-1.2)\approx0.8\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • Watch the signs in \(g_x=-f_x/f_z,\ g_y=-f_y/f_z\), and evaluate the partials at \((1,-1,1)\) (using \(z=g(1,-1)=1\)).
  • (a) The directional derivative in the direction of fastest increase is the number \(|\nabla g|\), not the vector \(\nabla g\).
  • (b) The increments are \(\Delta x=0.3,\ \Delta y=-0.2\) (mind the sign in \(y\)).
22 중간② 4번 합성함수의 2계 편도함수와 항등식Second partials of a composite and an identity15 pts §14.5
한국어 · 번역

\(g(s,t)=f\!\left(\dfrac ts,\ s^2+t^2\right)\)이고, \(f\)는 \(x\), \(y\)에 대한 미분가능한 함수로 \(f_x(0,1)=2\), \(f_y(0,1)=1\), \(f_{xx}(0,1)=-1\), \(f_{xy}(0,1)=3\), \(f_{yy}(0,1)=-2\)이다.

(a) \(g_s(1,0)\)과 \(g_{ss}(1,0)\)을 구하라.

(b) 다음을 만족하는 함수 \(h(x,y)\)를 구하라.

\[s\frac{\partial g}{\partial s}+t\frac{\partial g}{\partial t}=h(x,y)\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\]

\(x=\dfrac ts,\ y=s^2+t^2\)이라 하면 \(g(s,t)=f(x,y)\)이다. \((s,t)=(1,0)\)에서 \((x,y)=(0,1)\)이므로 \(f\)의 편도함수는 \((0,1)\)에서 평가한다. 중간변수의 편도함수는

\[x_s=-\frac{t}{s^2},\quad x_t=\frac1s,\quad y_s=2s,\quad y_t=2t,\quad x_{ss}=\frac{2t}{s^3},\quad y_{ss}=2.\]

\((1,0)\)에서 \(x_s=0,\ y_s=2,\ x_{ss}=0,\ y_{ss}=2\)이다.

(a)

연쇄법칙에 의해 \(g_s=f_x\,x_s+f_y\,y_s\)이므로 \(g_s(1,0)=2\cdot0+1\cdot2=2\).

\(g_s\)를 다시 \(s\)에 대해 미분하면(곱의 미분과 연쇄법칙, \(f_x,f_y\)도 \(x,y\)를 거쳐 \(s\)에 의존)

\[\begin{aligned}g_{ss}&=(f_{xx}x_s+f_{xy}y_s)\,x_s+f_x\,x_{ss}\\&\quad+(f_{yx}x_s+f_{yy}y_s)\,y_s+f_y\,y_{ss}.\end{aligned}\]

\((1,0)\)에서 \(x_s=x_{ss}=0\)이므로 첫 두 항과 \(f_{yx}x_s\)는 \(0\)이 되고

\[g_{ss}(1,0)=f_{yy}(0,1)\,y_s^2+f_y(0,1)\,y_{ss}=(-2)(4)+(1)(2)=-6.\]

답. \(g_s(1,0)=2\), \(g_{ss}(1,0)=-6\).

\[\begin{aligned}&\int_{1/2}^{1}\!\int_{-\sqrt3x}^{-\sqrt{1-x^2}} f(x,y)\,dy\,dx+\int_{1/2}^{1}\!\int_{\sqrt{1-x^2}}^{\sqrt3x} f(x,y)\,dy\,dx\\&\quad+\int_1^2\!\int_{-\sqrt{4-x^2}}^{\sqrt{4-x^2}} f(x,y)\,dy\,dx\end{aligned}\]

into one double integral.

(b) Find the center of mass above region if the density function is \(\rho(x,y)=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\).

(a)

Read off each region.

  • First: \(\frac12\le x\le1,\ -\sqrt3x\le y\le-\sqrt{1-x^2}\). The condition \(y\le-\sqrt{1-x^2}\) means \(x^2+y^2\ge1\) (with \(y\le0\)), and \(y\ge-\sqrt3x\) means above the ray \(\theta=-\frac\pi3\). The circle \(r=1\) meets the line \(y=-\sqrt3x\) at \(x=\frac12\), and the line meets \(x=1\) at \((1,-\sqrt3)\), a point on \(r=2\). So this is \(\{r\ge1,\ -\frac\pi3\le\theta\le0,\ x\le1\}\).
  • Second: the mirror image in the \(x\)-axis, \(\{r\ge1,\ 0\le\theta\le\frac\pi3,\ x\le1\}\) (\(y=\sqrt3x\) is \(\theta=\frac\pi3\)).
  • Third: \(1\le x\le2,\ |y|\le\sqrt{4-x^2}\), the part of the disk \(r\le2\) with \(x\ge1\). There \(\cos\theta=x/r\ge\frac12\), so \(|\theta|\le\frac\pi3\), and \(r\ge x\ge1\).

For fixed \(\theta\in[-\frac\pi3,\frac\pi3]\), the first two pieces give \(1\le r\le\sec\theta\) (\(x\le1\)) and the third gives \(\sec\theta\le r\le2\) (\(x\ge1\)); together, \(1\le r\le2\). The segment \(x=1\) is only a seam (figure). So the region is

\[D=\{(r,\theta)\mid -\tfrac\pi3\le\theta\le\tfrac\pi3,\ 1\le r\le2\}.\]

With \(dA=r\,dr\,d\theta\):

\[\int_{-\pi/3}^{\pi/3}\!\int_1^2 f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)\,r\,dr\,d\theta.\]

Answer. \(\displaystyle\int_{-\pi/3}^{\pi/3}\!\int_1^2 f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)\,r\,dr\,d\theta\).

(b)

The density is \(\rho=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=r\). The mass is

\[m=\iint_D\rho\,dA=\int_{-\pi/3}^{\pi/3}\!\int_1^2 r\cdot r\,dr\,d\theta=\frac{2\pi}{3}\cdot\frac73=\frac{14\pi}{9}.\]

\(D\) and \(\rho\) are symmetric about the \(x\)-axis and \(y\) is odd, so \(M_x=\iint_D y\rho\,dA=\int_{-\pi/3}^{\pi/3}\sin\theta\,d\theta\int_1^2r^3dr=0\), i.e. \(\bar y=0\).

\[\begin{aligned}M_y&=\iint_D x\rho\,dA=\int_{-\pi/3}^{\pi/3}\!\int_1^2 r\cos\theta\cdot r\cdot r\,dr\,d\theta\\&=\Bigl[\sin\theta\Bigr]_{-\pi/3}^{\pi/3}\cdot\Bigl[\frac{r^4}{4}\Bigr]_1^2=\sqrt3\cdot\frac{15}{4}=\frac{15\sqrt3}{4}.\end{aligned}\] \[\bar x=\frac{M_y}{m}=\frac{15\sqrt3}{4}\cdot\frac{9}{14\pi}=\frac{135\sqrt3}{56\pi}\approx1.329.\]

Answer. Center of mass \((\bar x,\bar y)=\left(\dfrac{135\sqrt3}{56\pi},\,0\right)\approx(1.329,\,0)\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • (a) Read each region in polar form (the lines \(y=\pm\sqrt3x\) are \(\theta=\pm\frac\pi3\)) and explain that \(x=1\) becomes a seam, so the union is \(1\le r\le2\). A sketch is required.

(a) 다음 적분을 \(dz\,dy\,dx\) 순서의 같은 값의 적분으로 바꾸어 써라.

\[\int_0^1\!\int_0^{1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}}\!\int_{-\sqrt{1-z}}^{\sqrt{1-z}} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dz\,dy\]

(b) 다음 적분을 구면좌표로 나타낸 같은 값의 적분으로 바꾸어라.

\[\int_0^{\pi}\!\int_0^{2\sin\theta}\!\int_{\sqrt{2r\sin\theta-r^2}}^{\sqrt{4-r^2}} f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta,z)\,r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta\]

(a)

주어진 적분의 영역은

\[E=\Bigl\{0\le y\le1,\ 0\le z\le1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2},\ |x|\le\sqrt{1-z}\Bigr\}.\]
  • \(z\le1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}\iff(y-1)^2+(z-1)^2\ge1\) (\(z\le1\)): \(yz\)평면에서 두 축과 원 \((y-1)^2+(z-1)^2=1\)의 호 사이 부분이다(\(y=0\)에서 \(z\le1\), \(y=1\)에서 \(z=0\)).
  • \(|x|\le\sqrt{1-z}\iff z\le1-x^2\) (포물기둥의 아래). 따라서 \(-1\le x\le1\).

즉 \(-1\le x\le1,\ 0\le y\le1\) 위에서 \(0\le z\le\min\{1-x^2,\ 1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}\}\)이다. 두 위쪽 곡면을 비교하면 (\(1-y\ge0\) 이용)

\[\begin{aligned}1-x^2\le1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}&\iff1-(y-1)^2\le x^4\\&\iff(1-y)^2\ge1-x^4\\&\iff y\le1-\sqrt{1-x^4}.\end{aligned}\]

따라서 \(xy\)평면 정사영 \([-1,1]\times[0,1]\)을 곡선 \(y=1-\sqrt{1-x^4}\)로 나눈다(그림).

① \(0\le y\le1-\sqrt{1-x^4}\): \(0\le z\le1-x^2\).  ② \(1-\sqrt{1-x^4}\le y\le1\): \(0\le z\le1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}\).

답. \(f=f(x,y,z)\)로 쓰면

\[\begin{aligned}&\int_{-1}^{1}\!\int_0^{1-\sqrt{1-x^4}}\!\int_0^{1-x^2}f\,dz\,dy\,dx\\&+\int_{-1}^{1}\!\int_{1-\sqrt{1-x^4}}^{1}\!\int_0^{1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}}f\,dz\,dy\,dx.\end{aligned}\]

(b)

주어진 영역은 \(0\le\theta\le\pi,\ 0\le r\le2\sin\theta,\ \sqrt{2r\sin\theta-r^2}\le z\le\sqrt{4-r^2}\)이고, \(f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta,z)\,r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta=f\,dV\)이다. 구면좌표 \(x=\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\ y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\ z=\rho\cos\phi\) (\(r=\rho\sin\phi\))로 각 경계를 바꾼다.

  • 아래 곡면 \(z=\sqrt{2y-x^2-y^2}\iff x^2+y^2+z^2=2y\ (z\ge0)\): \(\rho^2=2\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta\), 즉 \(\rho=2\sin\phi\sin\theta\). 조건은 \(\rho\ge2\sin\phi\sin\theta\).
  • 위 곡면 \(z=\sqrt{4-x^2-y^2}\iff\rho=2\ (z\ge0)\). 조건은 \(\rho\le2\).
  • 원기둥 \(r\le2\sin\theta\) (\(x^2+(y-1)^2\le1\)): \(\rho\sin\phi\le2\sin\theta\iff\rho\le\dfrac{2\sin\theta}{\sin\phi}\).

\[\begin{aligned}2\le\frac{2\sin\theta}{\sin\phi}&\iff\sin\phi\le\sin\theta\iff\phi\le\alpha(\theta),\\\alpha(\theta)&=\min\{\theta,\ \pi-\theta\}.\end{aligned}\]

즉 \(\phi\le\alpha(\theta)\)인 반직선은 구면 \(\rho=2\)로, \(\phi\ge\alpha(\theta)\)인 반직선은 원기둥 벽으로 빠져나간다(그림).

\(\phi\)의 구간이 \(\theta\)에 의존하므로 적분 순서는 \(d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\)로 쓴다.

답. \(F=f(\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\rho\cos\phi)\), \(\alpha(\theta)=\min\{\theta,\pi-\theta\}\)일 때

(\(\theta\)를 \([0,\frac\pi2]\), \([\frac\pi2,\pi]\)로 나누면 \(\alpha=\theta\), \(\alpha=\pi-\theta\).)

(a) Rewrite the integral as an equivalent integral in the \(dz\,dy\,dx\) order.

\[\int_0^1\!\int_0^{1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}}\!\int_{-\sqrt{1-z}}^{\sqrt{1-z}} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dz\,dy\]

(b) Convert the integral to an equivalent integral in spherical coordinates.

\[\int_0^{\pi}\!\int_0^{2\sin\theta}\!\int_{\sqrt{2r\sin\theta-r^2}}^{\sqrt{4-r^2}} f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta,z)\,r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta\]

(a)

The given integral is over

\[E=\Bigl\{0\le y\le1,\ 0\le z\le1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2},\ |x|\le\sqrt{1-z}\Bigr\}.\]
  • \(z\le1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}\iff(y-1)^2+(z-1)^2\ge1\) (with \(z\le1\)): in the \(yz\)-plane this is the part between the two axes and the arc of the circle \((y-1)^2+(z-1)^2=1\) (\(z\le1\) at \(y=0\), \(z=0\) at \(y=1\)).
  • \(|x|\le\sqrt{1-z}\iff z\le1-x^2\) (below a parabolic cylinder), hence \(-1\le x\le1\).

So over \(-1\le x\le1,\ 0\le y\le1\) we have \(0\le z\le\min\{1-x^2,\ 1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}\}\). Comparing the two top surfaces (using \(1-y\ge0\)):

\[\begin{aligned}1-x^2\le1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}&\iff1-(y-1)^2\le x^4\\&\iff(1-y)^2\ge1-x^4\\&\iff y\le1-\sqrt{1-x^4}.\end{aligned}\]

So the projection \([-1,1]\times[0,1]\) onto the \(xy\)-plane is split by the curve \(y=1-\sqrt{1-x^4}\) (figure).

① \(0\le y\le1-\sqrt{1-x^4}\): \(0\le z\le1-x^2\).  ② \(1-\sqrt{1-x^4}\le y\le1\): \(0\le z\le1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}\).

Answer. Writing \(f=f(x,y,z)\),

\[\begin{aligned}&\int_{-1}^{1}\!\int_0^{1-\sqrt{1-x^4}}\!\int_0^{1-x^2}f\,dz\,dy\,dx\\&+\int_{-1}^{1}\!\int_{1-\sqrt{1-x^4}}^{1}\!\int_0^{1-\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}}f\,dz\,dy\,dx.\end{aligned}\]

(b)

The given region is \(0\le\theta\le\pi,\ 0\le r\le2\sin\theta,\ \sqrt{2r\sin\theta-r^2}\le z\le\sqrt{4-r^2}\), and \(f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta,z)\,r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta=f\,dV\). Convert each boundary with \(x=\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\ y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\ z=\rho\cos\phi\) (\(r=\rho\sin\phi\)).

  • Bottom surface \(z=\sqrt{2y-x^2-y^2}\iff x^2+y^2+z^2=2y\ (z\ge0)\): \(\rho^2=2\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta\), i.e. \(\rho=2\sin\phi\sin\theta\). The condition is \(\rho\ge2\sin\phi\sin\theta\).
  • Top surface \(z=\sqrt{4-x^2-y^2}\iff\rho=2\ (z\ge0)\). The condition is \(\rho\le2\).
  • Cylinder \(r\le2\sin\theta\) (\(x^2+(y-1)^2\le1\)): \(\rho\sin\phi\le2\sin\theta\iff\rho\le\dfrac{2\sin\theta}{\sin\phi}\).
  • \(z\ge0\) gives \(0\le\phi\le\frac\pi2\), and \(0\le\theta\le\pi\).

For fixed \((\theta,\phi)\), \(\rho\) runs from \(2\sin\phi\sin\theta\) to \(\min\{2,\,2\sin\theta/\sin\phi\}\) (lower \(\le\) upper because \(\sin^2\phi\le1\)). Since \(\sin\) is increasing on \([0,\frac\pi2]\),

\[\begin{aligned}2\le\frac{2\sin\theta}{\sin\phi}&\iff\sin\phi\le\sin\theta\iff\phi\le\alpha(\theta),\\\alpha(\theta)&=\min\{\theta,\ \pi-\theta\}.\end{aligned}\]

So rays with \(\phi\le\alpha(\theta)\) leave through the sphere \(\rho=2\), and rays with \(\phi\ge\alpha(\theta)\) leave through the cylinder wall (figure).

Because the \(\phi\)-limits depend on \(\theta\), the order must be \(d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\).

Answer. With \(F=f(\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\rho\cos\phi)\) and \(\alpha(\theta)=\min\{\theta,\pi-\theta\}\),

\[\begin{aligned}&\int_0^{\pi}\!\int_0^{\alpha(\theta)}\!\int_{2\sin\phi\sin\theta}^{2}F\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\\&+\int_0^{\pi}\!\int_{\alpha(\theta)}^{\pi/2}\!\int_{2\sin\phi\sin\theta}^{\frac{2\sin\theta}{\sin\phi}}F\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta.\end{aligned}\]

(Splitting \(\theta\) into \([0,\frac\pi2]\) and \([\frac\pi2,\pi]\) gives \(\alpha=\theta\) and \(\alpha=\pi-\theta\).)

한국어 · 번역

\(E\)가 일엽쌍곡면 \(x^2+y^2-z^2=1\)의 내부에 있고, 평면 \(z=0\)의 위쪽, 포물면 \(z=3-x^2-y^2\)의 아래쪽에 있는 입체일 때 \(\displaystyle\iiint_E(1+x+y)\,dV\)를 계산하라.

원기둥좌표 \((r,\theta,z)\)를 쓴다. 일엽쌍곡면의 내부는 \(x^2+y^2-z^2\le1\), 즉 \(r^2\le1+z^2\)이므로

\[E=\{\,z\ge0,\ \ z\le3-r^2,\ \ r^2\le1+z^2\,\}.\]

쌍곡면과 포물면의 교선: \(1+z^2=3-z\iff z^2+z-2=(z+2)(z-1)=0\), \(z\ge0\)이므로 \(z=1,\ r=\sqrt2\). 따라서 고정된 \(r\)에서 \(z\)의 범위는(그림)

  • \(0\le r\le1\): \(r^2\le1+z^2\)이 자동이므로 \(0\le z\le3-r^2\).
  • \(1\le r\le\sqrt2\): \(\sqrt{r^2-1}\le z\le3-r^2\).
  • \(r>\sqrt2\): \(\sqrt{r^2-1}>1>3-r^2\)이므로 비어 있다.

피적분함수는 \(1+x+y=1+r\cos\theta+r\sin\theta\)이고, \(r,z\)의 범위가 \(\theta\)와 무관하므로 \(\int_0^{2\pi}\cos\theta\,d\theta=\int_0^{2\pi}\sin\theta\,d\theta=0\)에 의해 \(x,y\) 항은 \(0\)이다(\(E\)가 \(z\)축 대칭이고 \(x,y\)가 홀함수). 따라서 구하는 값은 \(E\)의 부피이다.

\[\begin{aligned}\iiint_E(1+x+y)\,dV&=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^{1}\!\int_0^{3-r^2}r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta\\&\quad+\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_1^{\sqrt2}\!\int_{\sqrt{r^2-1}}^{3-r^2}r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta\\&=2\pi\Bigl[\int_0^{\sqrt2}(3-r^2)\,r\,dr\\&\qquad-\int_1^{\sqrt2}\sqrt{r^2-1}\,r\,dr\Bigr]\\&=2\pi\Bigl(\Bigl[\tfrac32r^2-\tfrac14r^4\Bigr]_0^{\sqrt2}-\Bigl[\tfrac13(r^2-1)^{3/2}\Bigr]_1^{\sqrt2}\Bigr)\\&=2\pi\Bigl(2-\tfrac13\Bigr)=\frac{10\pi}{3}.\end{aligned}\]

(확인: 높이 \(z\)의 단면 넓이는 \(0\le z\le1\)에서 \(\pi(1+z^2)\), \(1\le z\le3\)에서 \(\pi(3-z)\)이므로 \(\frac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi=\frac{10\pi}{3}\).)

답. \(\dfrac{10\pi}{3}\).

English · 원문

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\iiint_E(1+x+y)\,dV\), where \(E\) is the solid that lies within the hyperboloid one-sheet \(x^2+y^2-z^2=1\), above the plane \(z=0\), and below the paraboloid \(z=3-x^2-y^2\).

Use cylindrical coordinates \((r,\theta,z)\). The inside of the hyperboloid of one sheet is \(x^2+y^2-z^2\le1\), i.e. \(r^2\le1+z^2\), so

\[E=\{\,z\ge0,\ \ z\le3-r^2,\ \ r^2\le1+z^2\,\}.\]

The hyperboloid meets the paraboloid where \(1+z^2=3-z\iff z^2+z-2=(z+2)(z-1)=0\); since \(z\ge0\), \(z=1,\ r=\sqrt2\). So for fixed \(r\) the \(z\)-range is (figure):

  • \(0\le r\le1\): \(r^2\le1+z^2\) holds automatically, so \(0\le z\le3-r^2\).
  • \(1\le r\le\sqrt2\): \(\sqrt{r^2-1}\le z\le3-r^2\).
  • \(r>\sqrt2\): \(\sqrt{r^2-1}>1>3-r^2\), so nothing.

The integrand is \(1+x+y=1+r\cos\theta+r\sin\theta\). The \(r,z\) limits do not depend on \(\theta\), and \(\int_0^{2\pi}\cos\theta\,d\theta=\int_0^{2\pi}\sin\theta\,d\theta=0\), so the \(x\) and \(y\) terms vanish (\(E\) is symmetric about the \(z\)-axis and \(x,y\) are odd). The integral equals the volume of \(E\):

\[\begin{aligned}\iiint_E(1+x+y)\,dV&=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^{1}\!\int_0^{3-r^2}r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta\\&\quad+\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_1^{\sqrt2}\!\int_{\sqrt{r^2-1}}^{3-r^2}r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta\\&=2\pi\Bigl[\int_0^{\sqrt2}(3-r^2)\,r\,dr\\&\qquad-\int_1^{\sqrt2}\sqrt{r^2-1}\,r\,dr\Bigr]\\&=2\pi\Bigl(\Bigl[\tfrac32r^2-\tfrac14r^4\Bigr]_0^{\sqrt2}-\Bigl[\tfrac13(r^2-1)^{3/2}\Bigr]_1^{\sqrt2}\Bigr)\\&=2\pi\Bigl(2-\tfrac13\Bigr)=\frac{10\pi}{3}.\end{aligned}\]

(Check by slices: the cross-section at height \(z\) has area \(\pi(1+z^2)\) for \(0\le z\le1\) and \(\pi(3-z)\) for \(1\le z\le3\), giving \(\frac{4\pi}{3}+2\pi=\frac{10\pi}{3}\).)

Answer. \(\dfrac{10\pi}{3}\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • Write "inside the hyperboloid" as \(r^2\le1+z^2\) and find the intersection \(z=1,\ r=\sqrt2\).
  • The lower \(z\)-limit changes from \(0\) to \(\sqrt{r^2-1}\) at \(r=1\). Using \(\sqrt{r^2-1}\le z\) on all of \(0\le r\le\sqrt2\) in a single integral is wrong.
  • Justify why the \(x,y\) terms vanish (the \(\theta\)-integral or symmetry). Do not drop the \(r\) in \(dV=r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta\).
22 중간② 10번 원뿔 위, 구면좌표 곡면 아래의 부피Volume above a cone, below a spherical-coordinate surface15 pts §15.8
한국어 · 번역

원뿔 \(z=\sqrt{\dfrac{x^2+y^2}{3}}\)의 위쪽, 곡면 \(x^2+y^2+z^2-\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}-z=0\)의 아래쪽에 있는 입체의 부피를 구하라.

구면좌표에서 \(x^2+y^2+z^2=\rho^2\), \(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=\rho\sin\phi\), \(z=\rho\cos\phi\)이다.

  • 원뿔: \(\rho\cos\phi=\dfrac{\rho\sin\phi}{\sqrt3}\iff\tan\phi=\sqrt3\iff\phi=\dfrac\pi3\). 원뿔의 위쪽(\(z\)축 쪽)은 \(0\le\phi\le\dfrac\pi3\).
  • 곡면: \(\rho^2-\rho-\rho\cos\phi=0\)이고 \(\rho>0\)이면 \(\rho=1+\cos\phi\). 이 곡면은 원점을 둘러싸는 닫힌 곡면이므로 그 아래(안쪽)는 \(0\le\rho\le1+\cos\phi\).

둘은 \(\phi=\frac\pi3\), \(\rho=\frac32\)에서 만난다(그림). 따라서

\[E=\{\,0\le\rho\le1+\cos\phi,\ \ 0\le\theta\le2\pi,\ \ 0\le\phi\le\tfrac\pi3\,\}.\] \[\begin{aligned}V&=\int_0^{\pi/3}\!\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^{1+\cos\phi}\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\theta\,d\phi\\&=2\pi\int_0^{\pi/3}\frac{(1+\cos\phi)^3}{3}\sin\phi\,d\phi.\end{aligned}\]

\(u=1+\cos\phi\)로 치환하면 \(du=-\sin\phi\,d\phi\), \(\phi=0\to u=2\), \(\phi=\frac\pi3\to u=\frac32\)이므로

\[\begin{aligned}V&=\frac{2\pi}{3}\int_{3/2}^{2}u^3\,du=\frac{2\pi}{3}\cdot\frac14\Bigl(16-\frac{81}{16}\Bigr)\\&=\frac\pi6\cdot\frac{175}{16}=\frac{175\pi}{96}.\end{aligned}\]

답. \(V=\dfrac{175\pi}{96}\approx5.727\).

채점 포인트 · 자주 하는 실수

  • 원뿔 \(z=\sqrt{(x^2+y^2)/3}\)를 \(\tan\phi=\sqrt3\), 즉 \(\phi=\frac\pi3\)로 바꾼다(\(\frac\pi6\)과 혼동 주의). "원뿔 위"는 \(0\le\phi\le\frac\pi3\).
  • 곡면을 \(\rho=1+\cos\phi\)로 정리하고 \(\rho\)의 범위 \(0\le\rho\le1+\cos\phi\)를 쓴다.
  • \(dV=\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\theta\,d\phi\)의 \(\sin\phi\)를 빠뜨리지 않고, 치환 \(u=1+\cos\phi\)의 한계 \(2\to\frac32\)와 부호를 처리한다.
English · 원문

Find the volume of the solid that lies above the cone \(z=\sqrt{\dfrac{x^2+y^2}{3}}\) and below the surface \(x^2+y^2+z^2-\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}-z=0\).

In spherical coordinates \(x^2+y^2+z^2=\rho^2\), \(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=\rho\sin\phi\), \(z=\rho\cos\phi\).

  • Cone: \(\rho\cos\phi=\dfrac{\rho\sin\phi}{\sqrt3}\iff\tan\phi=\sqrt3\iff\phi=\dfrac\pi3\). Above the cone (toward the \(z\)-axis) means \(0\le\phi\le\dfrac\pi3\).
  • Surface: \(\rho^2-\rho-\rho\cos\phi=0\), and for \(\rho>0\) this is \(\rho=1+\cos\phi\). It is a closed surface around the origin, so below (inside) it means \(0\le\rho\le1+\cos\phi\).

They meet at \(\phi=\frac\pi3\), \(\rho=\frac32\) (figure). Hence

\[E=\{\,0\le\rho\le1+\cos\phi,\ \ 0\le\theta\le2\pi,\ \ 0\le\phi\le\tfrac\pi3\,\}.\] \[\begin{aligned}V&=\int_0^{\pi/3}\!\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^{1+\cos\phi}\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\theta\,d\phi\\&=2\pi\int_0^{\pi/3}\frac{(1+\cos\phi)^3}{3}\sin\phi\,d\phi.\end{aligned}\]

Substitute \(u=1+\cos\phi\): \(du=-\sin\phi\,d\phi\), with \(\phi=0\to u=2\) and \(\phi=\frac\pi3\to u=\frac32\):

\[\begin{aligned}V&=\frac{2\pi}{3}\int_{3/2}^{2}u^3\,du=\frac{2\pi}{3}\cdot\frac14\Bigl(16-\frac{81}{16}\Bigr)\\&=\frac\pi6\cdot\frac{175}{16}=\frac{175\pi}{96}.\end{aligned}\]

Answer. \(V=\dfrac{175\pi}{96}\approx5.727\).

Key steps · Common mistakes

  • Convert the cone \(z=\sqrt{(x^2+y^2)/3}\) to \(\tan\phi=\sqrt3\), i.e. \(\phi=\frac\pi3\) (do not confuse with \(\frac\pi6\)). "Above the cone" means \(0\le\phi\le\frac\pi3\).
  • Rewrite the surface as \(\rho=1+\cos\phi\) and use \(0\le\rho\le1+\cos\phi\).
  • Keep the \(\sin\phi\) in \(dV=\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\theta\,d\phi\), and handle the limits \(2\to\frac32\) and the sign in the substitution \(u=1+\cos\phi\).
