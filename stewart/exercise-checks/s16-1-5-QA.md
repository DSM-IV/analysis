## Conditions made explicit

- 16.1.7–8 exclude the origin; 16.1.34 gradient does not exist at the origin.
- 16.2.29 uses the counterclockwise three-quarter arc, not the short quarter arc.
- 16.2.33 source exponents are `e^{-t}`, not `e^{2t}`.
- 16.2.43 source first component is `x-y^2`, not `x^2-y^2`.
- 16.2.48 standard numerical result assumes uniform ascent. Steady leakage in time without an ascent schedule is insufficient; this distinction is stated in the solution.
- 16.2.53 is explicitly a grid-based estimate, about 22 J; only components along the two straight legs contribute.
- 16.3.39 is a half-annulus (simply connected), not a full annulus.
- 16.4.9 and 14 remove exact single-coordinate differential terms before applying Green, avoiding boundary differentiability gaps.
- 16.4.12 inner boundary is clockwise; 13 has positive quarter-annulus orientation; 14–17 include the source orientation explicitly.
- 16.5 scalar/vector operation classification uses the textbook definition of grad, excluding tensor-valued grad F.
