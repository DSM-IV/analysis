문제 요약

해설.

  1. \(\text{(a)}\ f(0)<1\ne1.1\)

  2. \(\text{(b)}\ f^{\prime}(1)>0\ne-0.8\)

  3. \(\text{(c)}\ f^{\prime\prime}(2)<0\ne2!\cdot1.5=3\)

검산·주의점. 초기항·정의역과 극한 논증의 조건을 확인했다.

English

Problem summary

\(\text{(a)}\ 1.1+0.7x^2+2.2x^3+\cdots;\quad\text{(b)}\ 1.6-0.8(x-1)+0.4(x-1)^2-0.1(x-1)^3+\cdots;\quad\text{(c)}\ 2.8+0.5(x-2)+1.5(x-2)^2-0.1(x-2)^3+\cdots\) The graph has value below one at zero, increases at input one, and is concave down at input two. Final answer. \(\text{(a) wrong constant; (b) wrong slope sign; (c) wrong concavity sign}\)

문제 요약

\(f^{(n)}(4)=\frac{(-1)^nn!}{3^n(n+1)}\) 주어진 중심의 테일러 급수와 반지름을 구하라.

해설.

English

Problem summary

\(f^{(n)}(4)=\frac{(-1)^nn!}{3^n(n+1)}\) Find the Taylor series about the specified center and its radius.

Hint. Use derivatives at the center or substitutions into standard series, then check the convergence domain and remainder.

Solution.

  1. \(c_n=f^{(n)}(4)/n!=(-1)^n/[3^n(n+1)]\)

  2. \(|a_{n+1}/a_n|=|x-4|(n+1)/[3(n+2)]\to|x-4|/3\)

Final answer. \(\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{(-1)^n(x-4)^n}{3^n(n+1)},\quad R=3\)

Check and conditions. Initial terms, domain, and conditions of the limit argument were checked.

  2. \(|x|<1:\quad(1+x)g^{\prime}(x)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty[(n+1)c_{n+1}+nc_n]x^n=kg(x)\)

  3. \(h(x)=(1+x)^{-k}g(x),\quad h^{\prime}(x)=(1+x)^{-k}[g^{\prime}-kg/(1+x)]=0\)

  4. \(h(0)=1\Rightarrow h(x)=1\)

  5. The coefficient ratio justifies convergence and differentiation inside the unit interval. For a nonnegative integer exponent the series terminates and the same conclusion follows directly.

Final answer. \(\text{(a)} g^{\prime}=kg/(1+x);\quad\text{(b)} h^{\prime}=0;\quad\text{(c)} g(x)=(1+x)^k\quad(|x|<1)\)
