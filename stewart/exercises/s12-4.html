문제 요약

\(a=\langle2,3,0\rangle,\ b=\langle1,0,5\rangle\)의 외적을 구하고 두 벡터에 수직임을 확인하라.

힌트. 정의와 주어진 조건을 식으로 옮겨 단계별로 계산한다.

해설.

  1. \(a\times b=\langle a_2b_3-a_3b_2,a_3b_1-a_1b_3,a_1b_2-a_2b_1\rangle\)

  2. \((a\times b)\cdot a=(a\times b)\cdot b=0\)

최종 답. \(a\times b=\langle15,-10,-3\rangle\)

검산·주의점. 두 내적을 성분별로 전개해0임을 독립 확인했다.

English

Problem summary

Find the cross product for \(a=\langle2,3,0\rangle,\ b=\langle1,0,5\rangle\) and verify orthogonality.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. \(a\times b=\langle a_2b_3-a_3b_2,a_3b_1-a_1b_3,a_1b_2-a_2b_1\rangle\)

  2. \((a\times b)\cdot a=(a\times b)\cdot b=0\)

Final answer. \(a\times b=\langle15,-10,-3\rangle\)

Check and conditions. Both dot products were independently expanded and simplified to zero.
