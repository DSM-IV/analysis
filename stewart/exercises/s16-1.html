EXERCISE 1 벡터장과 흐름Vector fields and flowExercise 1 · p. 1167 · PDF p. 1204 개념 보기 · Concept
한국어

문제 요약

\(F=\langle1,1/2\rangle\)의 벡터장을 그려라.

힌트. 성분과 영점, 좌표축 위의 벡터를 먼저 계산한다.

해설.

  1. 점에 성분 \(\langle1,1/2\rangle\)를 대입한다.

  2. 모든 점에서 오른쪽으로 1, 위로 1/2인 화살표.

최종 답. 모든 점에서 오른쪽으로 1, 위로 1/2인 화살표. \(|F|=\sqrt5/2\).

검산·주의점. 위치에 의존하지 않으므로 방향과 길이가 일정하다.

English

Problem summary

Sketch the vector field \(F=\langle1,1/2\rangle\).

Hint. Start with components, zeros, and vectors on the coordinate axes.

Solution.

  1. Evaluate the components \(\langle1,1/2\rangle\) at each point.

  2. At every point draw an arrow 1 right and 1/2 up.

Final answer. At every point draw an arrow 1 right and 1/2 up. \(|F|=\sqrt5/2\).

Check and conditions. Both direction and length are independent of position.

English

Problem summary

Sketch the vector field \(F=\langle1,y/2\rangle\).

Hint. Start with components, zeros, and vectors on the coordinate axes.

Solution.

  1. Evaluate the components \(\langle1,y/2\rangle\) at each point.

English

Problem summary

Sketch the vector field \(F=\langle x,y/2\rangle\).

Hint. Start with components, zeros, and vectors on the coordinate axes.

Solution.

  1. Evaluate the components \(\langle x,y/2\rangle\) at each point.

  2. Zero at the origin; arrows point outward in each quadrant, horizontally on the x-axis and vertically on the y-axis.

Final answer. Zero at the origin; arrows point outward in each quadrant, horizontally on the x-axis and vertically on the y-axis. \(|F|=\sqrt{x^2+y^2/4}\).

Check and conditions. At (1,1), F=(1,1/2), so the field is not generally radial.

Problem summary

Sketch the vector field \(F=\langle y,x+y\rangle\).

Hint. Start with components, zeros, and vectors on the coordinate axes.

Solution.

문제 요약

힌트. 성분과 영점, 좌표축 위의 벡터를 먼저 계산한다.

해설.

  1. 점에 성분 \(\langle y,x\rangle/\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\)를 대입한다.

  2. 원점을 제외한 단위 벡터장. (1,0)에서 위, (0,1)에서 오른쪽이며 제1사분면에서 우상향한다.

English

Problem summary

Sketch the vector field \(F=\langle y,x\rangle/\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\).

Hint. Start with components, zeros, and vectors on the coordinate axes.

Solution.

  1. Evaluate the components \(\langle y,x\rangle/\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\) at each point.

  2. A unit field excluding the origin: upward at (1,0), rightward at (0,1), and northeast in quadrant I.

Final answer. A unit field excluding the origin: upward at (1,0), rightward at (0,1), and northeast in quadrant I. \(|F|=1\quad ((x,y)\ne(0,0))\).

Check and conditions. The squared components sum to 1; the origin is excluded.

English

Problem summary

Sketch the vector field \(F=\langle y,-x\rangle/\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\).

Hint. Start with components, zeros, and vectors on the coordinate axes.

Solution.

  1. Evaluate the components \(\langle y,-x\rangle/\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\) at each point.

Final answer. Clockwise unit arrows tangent to circles about the origin; exclude the origin. \(F\cdot\langle x,y\rangle=0,\quad |F|=1\).

Check and conditions. At (1,0), F=(0,-1), confirming clockwise rotation.
