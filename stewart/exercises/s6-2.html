문제 요약

밑b>0인 지수함수의(a)식,(b)정의역,(c)b≠1일 때 치역,(d)b>1,b=1,0<b<1인 그래프를 설명하라.

힌트. 정의와 주어진 조건을 식으로 옮겨 단계별로 계산한다.

해설.

  1. \[f(x)=b^x,\quad D=\mathbb R,\quad R=(0,\infty)\ (b\ne1)\]

  2. b>1이면 증가,0<b<1이면 감소하며(0,1)을 지난다. b=1이면 상수함수1이다.

최종 답. 식bˣ, 정의역ℝ; b≠1이면 치역(0,∞). b>1 증가,b=1 상수,0<b<1 감소.

검산·주의점. b⁰=1과 b^(-x)=1/bˣ로 절편과 반사 관계를 확인한다.

English

Problem summary

For an exponential function with base b>0, give(a)its equation,(b)domain,(c)range when b≠1,and(d)graphs for b>1,b=1,0<b<1.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. \[f(x)=b^x,\quad D=\mathbb R,\quad R=(0,\infty)\ (b\ne1)\]

  2. For b>1 it increases; for0<b<1 it decreases; both pass(0,1). For b=1 it is constantly1.

Final answer. Formula bˣ,domainℝ; range(0,∞) for b≠1. Increasing for b>1,constant for b=1,decreasing for0<b<1.

Check and conditions. b⁰=1 and b^(-x)=1/bˣ confirm the intercept and reflection relation.

English

Problem summary

Explain(a)the definition of e,(b)its approximate value,(c)the natural exponential function.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. \[\lim_{h\to0}\frac{e^h-1}{h}=1\]

  2. \[e\approx2.718281828459,\quad f(x)=e^x\]

힌트. 정의와 주어진 조건을 식으로 옮겨 단계별로 계산한다.

해설.

  1. \[y=3^{x} + 1\]

  2. \[\text{Key points: }(0,2)\]

  3. \[\text{Horizontal asymptote: }y=1\]

  4. 식을 구성하는 이동·반사를 적용한다. 절댓값 지수는 오른쪽 부분을 y축에 대칭시킨다.

English

Problem summary

\[y=3^{x} + 1\]Sketch using transformations of basic exponential graphs.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. \[y=3^{x} + 1\]

  2. \[\text{Key points: }(0,2)\]

  3. \[\text{Horizontal asymptote: }y=1\]

  4. Apply the shifts and reflections in the formula; an absolute-value exponent mirrors the right half across the y-axis.

Check and conditions. Checked intercepts by substitution and asymptotes by limits; the graph uses the original function values.
