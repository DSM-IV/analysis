문제 요약

(a)극장표 가격, (b)강의 흐름, (c)휴스턴에서 댈러스로 출발하는 비행 경로, (d)세계 인구를 스칼라 또는 벡터로 분류하라.

힌트. 정의와 주어진 조건을 식으로 옮겨 단계별로 계산한다.

해설.

  1. 가격과 인구는 수치 하나로 나타낸다. 흐름과 비행의 초기 이동은 크기와 방향이 필요하다.

최종 답. (a)스칼라 (b)벡터 (c)벡터 (d)스칼라.

검산·주의점. 방향 정보의 필요 여부로 구별한다.

English

Problem summary

Classify (a)a theater-ticket price, (b)a river current, (c)the initial Houston-to-Dallas flight path, (d)world population as scalar or vector.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. Price and population require only a number. Current and initial flight displacement require magnitude and direction.

Final answer. (a)Scalar (b)Vector (c)Vector (d)Scalar.

Check and conditions. The criterion is whether direction is required.

문제 요약

\((4,7)\)이라는 점과 \(\langle4,7\rangle\)이라는 벡터의 관계를 설명하고 그려라.

힌트. 정의와 주어진 조건을 식으로 옮겨 단계별로 계산한다.

해설.

  1. 점은 위치를, 벡터는 변위를 나타낸다. 벡터를 원점에 붙이면 끝점이(4,7)이다.

최종 답. \(\overrightarrow{OP}=\langle4,7\rangle,\quad P=(4,7)\)

검산·주의점. 같은 벡터는 평행이동하여 다른 시작점에서도 표현할 수 있다.

English

Problem summary

Relate and sketch the point \((4,7)\) and vector \(\langle4,7\rangle\).

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. A point specifies position; a vector specifies displacement. Placed at the origin, this vector ends at(4,7).

Final answer. \(\overrightarrow{OP}=\langle4,7\rangle,\quad P=(4,7)\)

Check and conditions. The same vector can be represented from any translated initial point.

문제 요약

원본 평행사변형 ABCD에서 대각선 교점을 E라 하자. 그림에 표시된 같은 벡터를 모두 찾아라.

힌트. 정의와 주어진 조건을 식으로 옮겨 단계별로 계산한다.

해설.

  1. 마주 보는 변은 방향과 길이가 같고, 대각선은 서로 이등분한다.

최종 답. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{DC},\ \overrightarrow{DA}=\overrightarrow{CB},\ \overrightarrow{DE}=\overrightarrow{EB},\ \overrightarrow{EA}=\overrightarrow{CE}\)

검산·주의점. 벡터는 길이가 같아도 방향이 반대이면 서로 같지 않다.

English

Problem summary

In the source parallelogram ABCD, E is the diagonal intersection. Find all equal directed vectors shown.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. Opposite sides have equal direction and length, and the diagonals bisect each other.

Final answer. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{DC},\ \overrightarrow{DA}=\overrightarrow{CB},\ \overrightarrow{DE}=\overrightarrow{EB},\ \overrightarrow{EA}=\overrightarrow{CE}\)

Check and conditions. Equal length alone is insufficient when directions are opposite.

문제 요약

그림의 A,B,C,D에 대해 (a)AB+BC, (b)CD+DB, (c)DB−AB, (d)DC+CA+AB를 단일 유향선분으로 나타내라(모두 벡터).

힌트. 정의와 주어진 조건을 식으로 옮겨 단계별로 계산한다.

해설.

  1. \((B-A)+(C-B)=C-A\)

  2. \((D-C)+(B-D)=B-C\)

  3. \((B-D)-(B-A)=A-D\)

  4. \((C-D)+(A-C)+(B-A)=B-D\)

최종 답. \((a)\ \overrightarrow{AC},\ (b)\ \overrightarrow{CB},\ (c)\ \overrightarrow{DA},\ (d)\ \overrightarrow{DB}\)

검산·주의점. 계산 결과를 주어진 벡터 조건에 대입해 확인한다.

English

Problem summary

Reduce (a)AB+BC, (b)CD+DB, (c)DB−AB, (d)DC+CA+AB to one directed segment, treating every segment as a vector.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. \((B-A)+(C-B)=C-A\)

  2. \((D-C)+(B-D)=B-C\)

  3. \((B-D)-(B-A)=A-D\)

  4. \((C-D)+(A-C)+(B-A)=B-D\)

Final answer. \((a)\ \overrightarrow{AC},\ (b)\ \overrightarrow{CB},\ (c)\ \overrightarrow{DA},\ (d)\ \overrightarrow{DB}\)

Check and conditions. Substitute the result into the stated vector conditions.

  2. 각 소문항의 그림에서 검은 화살표가 시작점부터 마지막 끝점까지의 합이다.

English

Problem summary

Using the displayed a(up),b(left),c(up-right), draw (a)a+b,(b)b+c,(c)a+c,(d)a−c,(e)b+a+c,(f)a−b−c.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. For a sum, place each next tail at the preceding tip. Reverse a vector before subtracting it.

  2. The black arrow in each panel runs from the first tail to the final tip.

Final answer. See the six head-to-tail constructions.

Check and conditions. Translations preserve each vector’s length and direction.

English

Problem summary

For u(up-right) and v(down-right), construct (a)u+v,(b)u−v,(c)2u,(d)−v/2,(e)3u+v,(f)v−2u.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. Scale lengths by the absolute coefficient and reverse directions for negative coefficients; then use head-to-tail addition.

Final answer. See the six constructions.

Check and conditions. Both2u and−v/2 remain parallel to their original vectors.

한국어

문제 요약

삼각형PQR에서 a=PQ,b=PR이고 M은 QR의 중점이다. c=PM,d=MR를 a,b로 나타내라(모두 벡터).

힌트. 정의와 주어진 조건을 식으로 옮겨 단계별로 계산한다.

해설.

  1. \(M=(Q+R)/2,\quad M-P=((Q-P)+(R-P))/2\)

  2. \(R-M=(R-Q)/2\)

최종 답. \(c=\frac{a+b}{2},\quad d=\frac{b-a}{2}\)

검산·주의점. c+d=b가 되어 PM+MR=PR와 일치한다.

English

Problem summary

In triangle PQR let a=PQ,b=PR, M the midpoint of QR, c=PM,d=MR (directed vectors). Express c,d using a,b.

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. \(M=(Q+R)/2,\quad M-P=((Q-P)+(R-P))/2\)

  2. \(R-M=(R-Q)/2\)

Final answer. \(c=\frac{a+b}{2},\quad d=\frac{b-a}{2}\)

Check and conditions. c+d=b agrees with PM+MR=PR.

Find |w| if \(|u|=|v|=1,\ u\perp v,\ u+v+w=0\).

Solution.

  1. \(w=-(u+v),\quad |w|^2=|u|^2+|v|^2=2\)

Final answer. \(|w|=\sqrt2\)

Check and conditions. This is the hypotenuse for perpendicular unit legs.

Problem summary

Describe \(r=\langle x,y\rangle,\quad |r-r_1|+|r-r_2|=k>|r_1-r_2|\).

Hint. Translate the definitions and conditions into equations, then work through them.

Solution.

  1. A constant sum of distances to two fixed foci defines an ellipse with semimajor axis k/2.

Check and conditions. k exceeds focal separation, so the semiminor axis is positive.
