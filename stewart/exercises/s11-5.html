EXERCISE 1 교대급수와 절대수렴Alternating series and absolute convergenceExercise 1 · p. 810 · PDF p. 847 개념 보기 · Concept
한국어

문제 요약

\(\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n,\quad b_n>0\) (a) 교대급수의 뜻 (b) 수렴의 충분조건 (c) 조건이 성립할 때 나머지의 성질을 설명하라.

힌트. 일반항과 부분합을 구별하고 등비급수·소거합·일반항의 영 수렴 조건을 살펴본다.

해설.

  1. \(\text{(a) successive signs alternate}\)

  2. \(\text{(b)}\ b_{n+1}\le b_n,\quad b_n\to0\)

  3. \(\text{(c)}\ |R_N|\le b_{N+1},\quad\operatorname{sgn}R_N=\operatorname{sgn}a_{N+1}\text{ unless }R_N=0\)

최종 답. \(\text{decreasing magnitudes tending to zero imply convergence and }|R_N|\le b_{N+1}\)

검산·주의점. 초기항·정의역과 극한 논증의 조건을 확인했다.

English

Problem summary

\(\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n,\quad b_n>0\) Explain (a) an alternating series, (b) sufficient conditions for convergence, and (c) the remainder under those conditions.

Hint. Distinguish terms from partial sums and examine geometric sums, telescoping, and the zero-term condition.

Solution.

  1. \(\text{(a) successive signs alternate}\)

  2. \(\text{(b)}\ b_{n+1}\le b_n,\quad b_n\to0\)

  3. \(\text{(c)}\ |R_N|\le b_{N+1},\quad\operatorname{sgn}R_N=\operatorname{sgn}a_{N+1}\text{ unless }R_N=0\)

Final answer. \(\text{decreasing magnitudes tending to zero imply convergence and }|R_N|\le b_{N+1}\)

Check and conditions. Initial terms, domain, and conditions of the limit argument were checked.

Problem summary

\(\sum a_n\text{ conditionally convergent},\quad r\in\mathbb R\) Prove that a rearrangement can have any prescribed real sum.

Hint. Distinguish terms from partial sums and examine geometric sums, telescoping, and the zero-term condition.

Solution.

  1. \(\sum a_n^+=+\infty,\quad\sum a_n^-=-\infty,\quad a_n\to0\)

  2. Take unused positive terms in their original order until first crossing above the target, then unused negative terms until first crossing below it. Divergence of each signed subseries makes every stage finite. Each overshoot is bounded by the magnitude of the last term of its block, hence tends to zero. Intermediate sums lie between the adjacent crossing values, so all partial sums approach the target. Both ordered sign lists are consumed indefinitely, using every nonzero term once; insert zero terms between blocks.

Final answer. \(\exists\text{ permutation }\sigma:\quad\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_{\sigma(n)}=r\)
