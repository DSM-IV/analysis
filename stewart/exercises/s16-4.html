  • b

  • a
  • b

For the positive boundary of type-II region \(D=\{(x,y):c\le y\le d,h_1(y)\le x\le h_2(y)\}\), prove \(\oint_CQ\,dy=\iint_DQ_xdA\), completing the special case of Green’s theorem.

Hint. Check orientation, then compute Qx−Py and the region bounds.

Solution.

  1. The right boundary runs c→d and the left d→c; horizontal pieces have dy=0. Thus the boundary integral is \(\int_c^d[Q(h_2(y),y)-Q(h_1(y),y)]dy\).

  2. For each y, the fundamental theorem writes the bracket as \(\int_{h_1(y)}^{h_2(y)}Q_x(x,y)dx\), yielding the double integral.

Final answer. \(\oint_CQ\,dy=\int_c^d\int_{h_1(y)}^{h_2(y)}Q_x\,dx\,dy\).

Check and conditions. The positive orientation traverses the right boundary upward, checking the sign.

Problem summary

Use Green’s theorem to prove the change-of-variables formula for f=1: \(\iint_Rdxdy=\iint_S|\partial(x,y)/\partial(u,v)|dudv\), where \(x=g(u,v),y=h(u,v)\) satisfies the regularity and injectivity hypotheses.

Hint. Check orientation, then compute Qx−Py and the region bounds.

Solution.

  1. First suppose J>0, so orientation is preserved: \(A(R)=\oint_{\partial R}x\,dy=\oint_{\partial S}(gh_u\,du+gh_v\,dv)\).

  2. Green’s theorem in the uv-plane gives \(\partial_u(gh_v)-\partial_v(gh_u)=g_uh_v-g_vh_u=J\).

  3. If J<0, orientation reverses and gives −J. Partitioning into regions of constant sign gives |J| in general.

Final answer. \(A(R)=\iint_S|g_uh_v-g_vh_u|\,du\,dv\).

Check and conditions. The mixed-partial terms ghvu and ghuv cancel, and the absolute value expresses orientation-independent area.
