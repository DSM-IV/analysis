문제 요약

C: \((x,y)=(t^2,2t),0\le t\le3\). \(\int_C y\,ds\)를 구하라.

힌트. 곡선을 매개화하고 ds와 dx,dy,dz를 구별한다.

해설.

  1. 매개화에서 \(ds=2\sqrt{t^2+1}\,dt\).

  2. 대입하여 \(\int_0^3 4t\sqrt{t^2+1}dt=(40\sqrt{10}-4)/3\).

최종 답. \((40\sqrt{10}-4)/3\).

검산·주의점. 원문의 끝점·구간을 대조하고 매개화의 미분으로 적분함수를 재계산했다.

English

Problem summary

For C: \((x,y)=(t^2,2t),0\le t\le3\), evaluate \(\int_C y\,ds\).

Hint. Parametrize the curve and distinguish ds from dx,dy,dz.

Solution.

  1. The parametrization gives \(ds=2\sqrt{t^2+1}\,dt\).

  2. Substitution yields \(\int_0^3 4t\sqrt{t^2+1}dt=(40\sqrt{10}-4)/3\).

Final answer. \((40\sqrt{10}-4)/3\).

Check and conditions. The source endpoints and interval were checked, and differentiating the parametrization independently reproduced the integrand.

힌트. 곡선을 매개화하고 ds와 dx,dy,dz를 구별한다.

English

Problem summary

Evaluate \(\int_C(x+2y)dx+x^2dy\) along the two segments (0,0)→(2,1)→(3,0).

Hint. Parametrize the curve and distinguish ds from dx,dy,dz.

Solution.

  1. The first segment \((2t,t),0\le t\le1\) gives \(\int_0^1(8t+4t^2)dt=16/3\).

  2. The second segment \((2+t,1-t)\) gives \(\int_0^1(-5t-t^2)dt=-17/6\). Add the two values.

Check and conditions. The second segment has dy=−dt, accounting for its negative contribution.

문제 요약

\(\int_Cx^2dx+y^2dy\). C는 (2,0)에서 (0,2)까지 반시계 사분원 \(x^2+y^2=4\) 후 (−1,1)까지 선분이다.

English

Problem summary

Evaluate \(\int_Cx^2dx+y^2dy\), where C follows the counterclockwise quarter-circle \(x^2+y^2=4\) from (2,0) to (0,2), then a segment to (−1,1).

Hint. Parametrize the curve and distinguish ds from dx,dy,dz.

Solution.

  1. The form is exact: \(x^2dx+y^2dy=d[(x^3+y^3)/3]\).

  2. The arc endpoints both have potential 8/3, contributing zero; the segment contributes \(0-8/3\).

Final answer. −8/3.

Check and conditions. At (−1,1), the sum of the coordinate cubes is zero.
