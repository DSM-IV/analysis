해설.

  2. \[u=1+x^2,\ du=2xdx\]

  3. \[F(x)=\frac{\log{\left(x^{2} + 1 \right)}}{2}\]

  8. \[\frac1{1-x^2}=\frac1{2(1+x)}+\frac1{2(1-x)}\]
