힌트. 직교좌표식 또는 반지름 조건으로 바꾼다.

English

Problem summary

Describe the surface \(\phi=3\pi/4\).

Hint. Convert to a rectangular equation or radial condition.

Solution.

  1. \(r=\rho\sin\phi=\rho/\sqrt2\) and \(z=\rho\cos\phi=-\rho/\sqrt2\), so z=-r.

Final answer. \(z=-\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\): the lower cone with half-angle45° about the negative z-axis.
