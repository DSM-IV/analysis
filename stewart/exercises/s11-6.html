한국어

문제 요약

\(L=\lim|a_{n+1}/a_n|\) 극한이 각각 (a) 팔 (b) 팔십 퍼센트 (c) 일일 때 급수에 대해 설명하라.

힌트. 일반항과 부분합을 구별하고 등비급수·소거합·일반항의 영 수렴 조건을 살펴본다.

해설.

  1. \(L>1\Rightarrow\text{divergence};\quad L<1\Rightarrow\text{absolute convergence}\)

  2. \(L=1:\quad\sum1/n\text{ diverges},\quad\sum1/n^2\text{ converges}\)

최종 답. \(\text{(a) divergent; (b) absolutely convergent; (c) inconclusive}\)

검산·주의점. 초기항·정의역과 극한 논증의 조건을 확인했다.

English

Problem summary

\(L=\lim|a_{n+1}/a_n|\) Describe the conclusion when the ratio limit is (a) eight, (b) eight tenths, and (c) one.

Hint. Distinguish terms from partial sums and examine geometric sums, telescoping, and the zero-term condition.

Solution.

  1. \(L>1\Rightarrow\text{divergence};\quad L<1\Rightarrow\text{absolute convergence}\)

  2. \(L=1:\quad\sum1/n\text{ diverges},\quad\sum1/n^2\text{ converges}\)

Final answer. \(\text{(a) divergent; (b) absolutely convergent; (c) inconclusive}\)

Check and conditions. Initial terms, domain, and conditions of the limit argument were checked.
