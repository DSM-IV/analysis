한국어

문제 요약

원본 격자 곡선에서 f′(0),f′(1),f′(2),f′(3),f′(4),f′(5),f′(6),f′(7)를 순서대로 추정하고 f′ 그래프를 그려라. 자체 도해의 위 패널은 원본 곡선의 근사 재구성이다.

힌트. 접선 기울기의 부호·크기를 읽거나 차분몫의 극한을 계산한다.

해설.

  1. 도함수 그래프의 높이는 원래 그래프의 높이가 아니라 접선 기울기이다. 원래 함수가 증가하면 도함수는 양수, 감소하면 음수이고, 수평 접선에서는 존재하는 도함수가 0이다. 기울기가 점점 커지는 구간은 도함수 그래프가 올라가는 구간과 대응한다.

  2. 각 점의 접선에 작은 수평 이동을 잡고 그에 따른 수직 이동을 나눈다. 극대·극소점은 기울기0이다.

  3. f′(0)≈0.5, f′(1)≈0, f′(2)≈-1, f′(3)≈-1.5, f′(4)≈-1, f′(5)≈0, f′(6)≈1, f′(7)≈1.2

  4. 도함수 점들을 원함수의 증감·굽음에 맞춰 부드럽게 연결한다. 수치는 대략 ±0.2–0.3 수준의 그래프 추정이다.

최종 답. f′(0)≈0.5, f′(1)≈0, f′(2)≈-1, f′(3)≈-1.5, f′(4)≈-1, f′(5)≈0, f′(6)≈1, f′(7)≈1.2

검산·주의점. 도함수 영점 1,5는 원본의 극값 위치와 일치하고 증가/감소 구간에서 도함수 부호도 일치한다.

English

Problem summary

Estimate f′(0),f′(1),f′(2),f′(3),f′(4),f′(5),f′(6),f′(7) in order from the source grid curve, then sketch f′. The upper panel reconstructs the source approximately.

Hint. Read the sign and size of tangent slopes, or calculate a difference-quotient limit.

Solution.

  1. The derivative graph’s height records slope, not the original function’s height. Increasing and decreasing portions give positive and negative derivative values; a horizontal tangent gives derivative zero where it exists. Increasing slopes correspond to a rising derivative graph.

  2. At each point divide the rise along the tangent by a small horizontal run. Slopes vanish at local maxima and minima.

  3. f′(0)≈0.5, f′(1)≈0, f′(2)≈-1, f′(3)≈-1.5, f′(4)≈-1, f′(5)≈0, f′(6)≈1, f′(7)≈1.2

  4. Connect the derivative estimates smoothly while respecting the original monotonicity and curvature. Numerical slopes carry roughly ±0.2–0.3 graphical uncertainty.

Final answer. f′(0)≈0.5, f′(1)≈0, f′(2)≈-1, f′(3)≈-1.5, f′(4)≈-1, f′(5)≈0, f′(6)≈1, f′(7)≈1.2

Check and conditions. The derivative zeros 1,5 match the original extrema, and derivative signs agree with increase/decrease.

검산·주의점. 각 대응에서 함수 극값과 도함수 영점, 직선 모서리와 도함수 구멍을 함께 대조했다.

English

Problem summary

Match four function sketches with derivative sketches I–IV: (a)a decreasing–increasing–decreasing cubic shape, (b)rising–falling–rising line pieces, (c)a negative valley flattening at both ends, (d)an inverted W with two maxima and a central minimum. The candidates are I(negative trough then positive hump),II(downward parabola),III(negative cubic with three zeros),IV(positive–negative–positive constant pieces).

Hint. Read the sign and size of tangent slopes, or calculate a difference-quotient limit.

  2. The two extrema of(a)match the two zeros ofII. Constant piecewise slopes in(b)giveIV, with corner arguments omitted.

  3. Curve(c)decreases left, increases right, and flattens at both ends, givingI. Curve(d)has derivative signs +,−,+,−, givingIII.

Final answer. (a)II; (b)IV; (c)I; (d)III.

Check and conditions. For every match, checked function extrema against derivative zeros, and line corners against missing derivative values.

English

Problem summary

Source shape: A symmetric smooth valley: decreasing on the left, minimal at zero, increasing on the right, and almost flat at both ends. Reconstruct f using equally scaled axes and sketch f′ below it.

Hint. Read the sign and size of tangent slopes, or calculate a difference-quotient limit.

  2. Transfer horizontal-tangent arguments to derivative zeros and mark increasing/decreasing portions positive/negative. Do not assign finite derivatives at corners or vertical tangents.

  3. The derivative has a negative trough left, zero at the origin, a positive hump right, and tends toward zero at the ends.

Final answer. The derivative has a negative trough left, zero at the origin, a positive hump right, and tends toward zero at the ends.

Check and conditions. The source axes have no numerical ticks, so numbers in the original illustration normalize position and scale. Checked derivative signs and all extrema/corners against the source.
