  4. \[A\prime(t)=\tfrac12(\cosh^2t-\sinh^2t)=\tfrac12,\quad A(0)=0\]

Final answer. \[A(t)=t/2,\quad t\ge0\]

Check and conditions. Used the positive parameter orientation. For negative t, unsigned geometric area is |t|/2 while oriented area is t/2.
