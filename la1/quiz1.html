한눈에 보는 핵심 12 · Must-Know 12

  1. Vector space = 집합 $V$ + 덧셈 $+:V\times V\to V$ + 스칼라곱 $\cdot:\mathbb{R}\times V\to V$, 공리 (VS 1)–(VS 8)을 만족.
  2. Cancellation Law $x+z=y+z\Rightarrow x=y$ → 영벡터와 덧셈 역원은 유일, $0\cdot v=0_V$, $a\cdot 0_V=0_V$, $(-a)v=-(av)=a(-v)$.
  3. Subspace Criterion: $W\subseteq V$가 부분공간 $\iff$ (1) $0_V\in W$ (2) 덧셈에 닫힘 (3) 스칼라곱에 닫힘.
  4. $W_1\cap W_2$는 항상 부분공간, $W_1\cup W_2$는 일반적으로 아니다(x축 ∪ y축).
  5. $\operatorname{span}(S)$ = $S$의 (유한) 일차결합 전체 = $S$를 포함하는 가장 작은 부분공간. $\operatorname{span}(\varnothing)=\{0\}$.
  6. l.d.: 자명하지 않은(계수가 전부 0은 아닌) 일차결합이 $0$. l.i.: $\sum a_iv_i=0\Rightarrow a_i=0\ \forall i$. $\varnothing$은 l.i.
  7. 리스트에 같은 벡터가 두 번 있거나 영벡터가 있으면 l.d.; $v_2=cv_1$이면 l.d.
  8. $S_1\subseteq S_2$: $S_1$ l.d. $\Rightarrow$ $S_2$ l.d. / $S_2$ l.i. $\Rightarrow$ $S_1$ l.i.
  9. $S$ l.d. $\iff$ 빼도 span이 그대로인 벡터 $v\in S$가 존재 (그리고 $\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})=\operatorname{span}(S)\iff v\in\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})$).
  10. $S$ l.i.이고 $v\notin\operatorname{span}(S)$ $\Rightarrow$ $S\cup\{v\}$ l.i.
  11. Basis = l.i. + spanning $\iff$ 모든 $w$가 유일한 일차결합으로 표현됨.
  12. 유한 생성집합은 기저를 포함하고, Replacement Thm에 의해 유한 기저들의 원소 수는 모두 같다.

§1.1 ℝⁿ and the Axioms · ℝⁿ과 벡터공간 공리

DEF 1.3 · 1.4 ℝⁿ과 두 연산 ℝⁿ, addition, scalar multiplication

$\mathbb{R}^n := \{(x_1,\dots,x_n) : x_i\in\mathbb{R}\ \text{for every } i=1,\dots,n\}$; the $k$-th coordinate of $(x_1,\dots,x_n)$ is $x_k$.

$(x_1,\dots,x_n)+(y_1,\dots,y_n) := (x_1+y_1,\dots,x_n+y_n)$,   $c\,(x_1,\dots,x_n) := (cx_1,\dots,cx_n)$,   $v-w := v+(-1)w$.

Cartesian product: $X\times Y := \{(x,y): x\in X,\ y\in Y\}$, so $+:\mathbb{R}^n\times\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^n$ and $\cdot:\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^n$ are functions.

이 수업은 ℝⁿ을 열벡터의 공간으로 본다(Notation 1.7): $\begin{bmatrix}x_1\\ \vdots\\ x_n\end{bmatrix}:=(x_1,\dots,x_n)$, 즉 $\mathbb{R}^n=M_{n\times 1}(\mathbb{R})$. 쉼표 있는 $n$-튜플 = 열벡터 ≠ 쉼표 없는 행벡터 $[a_1\ \cdots\ a_n]\in M_{1\times n}(\mathbb{R})$.

PROP 1.6 · DEF 1.9 벡터공간 공리 — 영어로 쓸 수 있어야 함 Axioms of a vector space

Def. A vector space $V$ (over $\mathbb{R}$) is a set with addition $+:V\times V\to V$ and scalar multiplication $\cdot:\mathbb{R}\times V\to V$ satisfying:

(VS 1) $\forall v,w\in V$, $v+w=w+v$.

(VS 2) $\forall u,v,w\in V$, $(u+v)+w=u+(v+w)$.

(VS 3) There exists $0_V\in V$ such that $\forall v\in V$, $v+0_V=v=0_V+v$.  (zero vector)

(VS 4) $\forall v\in V$, $\exists w\in V$ s.t. $v+w=0_V$.  (additive inverse of $v$)

(VS 5) $\forall v\in V$, $1\cdot v=v$.

(VS 6) $\forall a,b\in\mathbb{R}$ and $\forall v\in V$, $(ab)\cdot v=a\cdot(b\cdot v)$.

(VS 7) $\forall c\in\mathbb{R}$ and $\forall v,w\in V$, $c(v+w)=cv+cw$.

(VS 8) $\forall a,b\in\mathbb{R}$ and $\forall v\in V$, $(a+b)v=av+bv$.

Elements of $V$ are called vectors.

외우는 법: 덧셈 4개(교환·결합·영벡터·역원) + 스칼라곱 4개($1v=v$, 곱의 결합, 분배 두 개). 번호는 안 외워도 되지만 내용은 영어로 정확히 쓸 수 있어야 한다. Prop 1.6: ℝⁿ은 이 8개를 만족한다(좌표별 연산이므로 $n=1$, 즉 실수의 성질로 환원).

공리 하나를 증명하는 법 (예: ℝⁿ의 (VS 7))

Let $c\in\mathbb{R}$ and $v=(v_1,\dots,v_n)$, $w=(w_1,\dots,w_n)\in\mathbb{R}^n$. Then

$c(v+w)=c(v_1+w_1,\dots,v_n+w_n)=(cv_1+cw_1,\dots,cv_n+cw_n)=(cv_1,\dots,cv_n)+(cw_1,\dots,cw_n)=cv+cw,$

where the middle equality uses the distributive law in $\mathbb{R}$ coordinate by coordinate. $\square$

반례 (Ex 1.14): ℝ²에 덧셈을 $(a,b)+'(c,d):=(a+c,0)$로 바꾸면 (VS 3)이 깨진다.

For $v=(1,1)$: $\forall (c,d)\in\mathbb{R}^2$, $(1,1)+'(c,d)=(1+c,0)\neq(1,1)$, so there is no zero vector $0_{\mathbb{R}^2}$ for $(1,1)$. Hence $(\mathbb{R}^2,+',\cdot)$ is not a vector space.

함수공간 공리 증명 틀 (Ex 1.8, (VS 1))

For every $f,g\in\mathcal{F}(X,\mathbb{R})$ and every $x\in X$, $(f+g)(x)=f(x)+g(x)=g(x)+f(x)=(g+f)(x)$ (VS 1 in $\mathbb{R}$), so $f+g=g+f$. $\square$

두 함수가 같다 = 모든 입력에서 값이 같다. 다른 공리도 같은 방식: 함숫값(실수)에서 성립하는 성질로 환원.

LEM 1.15 · COR 1.16 · PROP 1.17 벡터공간의 기본 성질 Properties of a vector space

Cancellation Law. Let $x,y,z\in V$. If $x+z=y+z$, then $x=y$.

Uniqueness. (1) $V$ has a unique zero vector. (2) Every $v\in V$ has a unique additive inverse, denoted $-v$.

Prop. $\forall v\in V$, $\forall a\in\mathbb{R}$: (1) $0\cdot v=0_V$; (2) $(-a)\cdot v=-(av)=a(-v)$, in particular $-v=(-1)\cdot v$; (3) $a\cdot 0_V=0_V$.

증명: Cancellation Law (강의노트 증명)

Let $w$ be an additive inverse of $z$ (VS 4). Then $x+z=y+z \Rightarrow (x+z)+w=(y+z)+w \overset{\text{VS 2}}{\Rightarrow} x+(z+w)=y+(z+w) \overset{\text{VS 4}}{\Rightarrow} x+0_V=y+0_V \overset{\text{VS 3}}{\Rightarrow} x=y.$ $\square$

증명: 영벡터·역원의 유일성

(1) Suppose $0_V$ and $0'_V$ both satisfy (VS 3). Since $0'_V$ is a zero vector, $0_V+0'_V=0_V$; since $0_V$ is a zero vector, $0_V+0'_V=0'_V$. Hence $0_V=0'_V$.

(2) Suppose $w_1,w_2$ are additive inverses of $v$. Then $v+w_1=0_V=v+w_2$, so by (VS 1) $w_1+v=w_2+v$, and the Cancellation Law gives $w_1=w_2$. $\square$

증명: $0\cdot v=0_V$, $a\cdot0_V=0_V$, $(-a)v=-(av)$

(1) $0v+0v=(0+0)v=0v=0v+0_V$ by (VS 8) and (VS 3); by the Cancellation Law (after (VS 1)), $0v=0_V$.

(3) $a\cdot0_V+0_V=a\cdot0_V=a(0_V+0_V)=a\cdot0_V+a\cdot0_V$ by (VS 3), (VS 3), (VS 7); cancelling $a\cdot 0_V$ gives $0_V=a\cdot0_V$. (This is the lecture's proof.)

(2) $av+(-a)v=(a+(-a))v=0v=0_V$ by (VS 8) and (1), so $(-a)v$ is an additive inverse of $av$; by uniqueness, $(-a)v=-(av)$. Also $av+a(-v)=a(v+(-v))=a\cdot0_V=0_V$ by (VS 7) and (3), so $a(-v)=-(av)$. Taking $a=1$ and using (VS 5): $(-1)v=-v$. $\square$

DEF 1.18 · THM 1.20 부분공간과 판정법 — 가장 자주 쓰는 정리 Subspace & Subspace Criterion

Def. Let $V$ be a vector space. $W\subseteq V$ is a subspace of $V$ if $W$ with the induced $+$ and $\cdot$ from $V$ is a vector space.

Subspace Criterion. Let $V$ be a vector space and $W\subseteq V$. Then $W$ is a subspace of $V$ if and only if

(1) $0_V\in W$;  (2) $x+y\in W$ for all $x,y\in W$;  (3) $cx\in W$ for all $c\in\mathbb{R}$ and all $x\in W$.

$V$와 $\{0\}$(자명한 부분공간)은 항상 부분공간. 부분공간의 영벡터는 $0_W=0_V$, $W$ 안의 역원도 $-x=(-1)x$로 $V$의 것과 같다(Rmk 1.21). 아닌 걸 보일 때는 반례 하나면 충분 — 먼저 $0\in W$인지부터 확인.

모범 증명 틀: Ex 1.22 $W=\{(a_1,\dots,a_n)\in\mathbb{R}^n:\sum a_i=0\}$은 부분공간

We check the three conditions of the Subspace Criterion.

(1) $0_{\mathbb{R}^n}=(0,\dots,0)$ satisfies $\sum_{i=1}^n 0=0$, so $0_{\mathbb{R}^n}\in W$.

(2) Let $(a_1,\dots,a_n),(b_1,\dots,b_n)\in W$, so $\sum a_i=0=\sum b_i$. Then $\sum(a_i+b_i)=\sum a_i+\sum b_i=0+0=0$, hence $(a_1,\dots,a_n)+(b_1,\dots,b_n)=(a_1+b_1,\dots,a_n+b_n)\in W$.

(3) Let $c\in\mathbb{R}$ and $(a_1,\dots,a_n)\in W$. Then $\sum ca_i=c\sum a_i=c\cdot0=0$, so $c(a_1,\dots,a_n)=(ca_1,\dots,ca_n)\in W$.

Therefore $W$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$. $\square$

반례 틀: Ex 1.23 $W=\{(a,b): a=0 \text{ or } b=0\}$은 부분공간이 아님

$(1,0),(0,1)\in W$, but $(1,0)+(0,1)=(1,1)\notin W$ by definition of $W$. This violates condition (2) of the Subspace Criterion, so $W$ is not a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$.

다른 전형적 반례: 직선 $y=x-1$은 $(0,0)\notin W$로 (1) 위반 · 제1사분면 $\{(a,b):a,b\ge0\}$은 $(1,1)\in W$이지만 $(-1)(1,1)\notin W$로 (3) 위반.

EXAM 1.24–1.26 다항식·연속함수 부분공간 Polynomials and continuous functions

$\mathbb{R}[x]=\left\{\sum_{i=0}^d a_ix^i : d\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq0},\ a_i\in\mathbb{R}\right\}\subseteq\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})$ is a subspace.

For $f=\sum_{i=0}^d a_ix^i\neq0$, $\deg(f):=\max\{i\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq0}: a_i\neq0\}$, and $\deg(0):=-\infty$.

$\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}:=\left\{\sum_{i=0}^n a_ix^i : a_i\in\mathbb{R}\right\}\subsetneq\mathbb{R}[x]$ (polynomials of degree $\leq n$, including $0$) is a subspace. $C(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})$ (continuous functions) is a subspace of $\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})$.

증명: $\mathbb{R}[x]$는 부분공간 (강의노트 증명 — 차수 맞추기 트릭)

(1) $0_{\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})}$ (the function $c\mapsto0$) equals $0=0\cdot x^0\in\mathbb{R}[x]$.

(2) Given $\sum_{i=0}^{d_1}a_ix^i$, $\sum_{i=0}^{d_2}b_ix^i\in\mathbb{R}[x]$, let $d:=\max(d_1,d_2)$, $a_j:=0$ for $d_1\lt j\leq d$ and $b_j:=0$ for $d_2\lt j\leq d$. Then $\sum_{i=0}^{d_1}a_ix^i+\sum_{i=0}^{d_2}b_ix^i=\sum_{i=0}^{d}(a_i+b_i)x^i\in\mathbb{R}[x]$.

(3) $c\cdot\sum_{i=0}^d a_ix^i=\sum_{i=0}^d(ca_i)x^i\in\mathbb{R}[x]$. $\square$

오타 주의 Ex 1.25 힌트의 "$\deg(fg)=\deg(f)\deg(g)$"는 틀림 → 올바르게는 $\deg(fg)=\deg(f)+\deg(g)$. 부분공간 증명에는 $\deg(f+g)\leq\max(\deg f,\deg g)$, $\deg(cf)\leq\deg f$만 있으면 된다.

PROP 1.28 · REM 1.29 교집합은 부분공간, 합집합은 아님 Intersections of subspaces

Let $W_1,W_2\subseteq V$ be subspaces. Then $W_1\cap W_2$ is also a subspace of $V$. More generally, $W_1\cap\cdots\cap W_m$ is a subspace, and for any collection $\mathcal{C}\subseteq\mathcal{P}(V)$ of subspaces, $\bigcap_{W\in\mathcal{C}}W:=\{x\in V: x\in W\ \forall W\in\mathcal{C}\}$ is a subspace.

증명 (강의노트)

It suffices to check the Subspace Criterion on $W_1\cap W_2$.

(1) $W_1,W_2$ are subspaces $\Rightarrow$ $0_V\in W_1$ and $0_V\in W_2$ by the Subspace Criterion $\Rightarrow$ $0_V\in W_1\cap W_2$.

(2) For $x,y\in W_1\cap W_2$: since $W_1\cap W_2\subseteq W_i$, we have $x,y\in W_i$ for $i=1,2$. The Subspace Criterion on $W_1,W_2$ gives $x+y\in W_1$ and $x+y\in W_2$, so $x+y\in W_1\cap W_2$.

(3) For $c\in\mathbb{R}$ and $x\in W_1\cap W_2$: $x\in W_i$ for $i=1,2$, so $cx\in W_1$ and $cx\in W_2$, hence $cx\in W_1\cap W_2$. $\square$

ℝ³에서 $xy$-평면 ∩ $xz$-평면 = $x$축(부분공간). 반면 $W_1\cup W_2$는 Ex 1.23처럼 부분공간이 아닐 수 있다.

§1.3 Linear Combinations, Span, Linear Independence · 일차결합, 생성, 일차독립

DEF 1.30 · 1.31 · 1.34 일차결합과 생성 Linear combination, span, spanning set

(1) $v\in V$ is a linear combination of $w_1,\dots,w_k\in V$ if $\exists a_1,\dots,a_k\in\mathbb{R}$ s.t. $v=\sum_{i=1}^k a_iw_i$ ($a_i$: coefficients).

(2) $v$ is a l.c. of $S$ ($\varnothing\neq S\subseteq V$) if $\exists$ a finite subset $\varnothing\neq T=\{w_1,\dots,w_k\}\subseteq S$ s.t. $v$ is a l.c. of $w_1,\dots,w_k$.

(3) $v$ is a l.c. of $\varnothing$ if $v=0$.

$\operatorname{span}(w_1,\dots,w_k):=\left\{\sum_{i=1}^k a_iw_i : a_i\in\mathbb{R}\ \forall i\right\}$,   $\operatorname{span}(S):=\{v\in V: v \text{ is a l.c. of } S\}$.

A spanning set of a subspace $W$ is $S\subseteq W$ with $W=\operatorname{span}(S)$; we say $S$ spans (generates) $W$.

일차결합은 언제나 유한개의 합. 정의 (3) 때문에 $\operatorname{span}(\varnothing)=\{0\}$. $(w_1,\dots,w_k)$는 순서·중복을 허용하는 리스트($\in V^k$)로 보지만 span은 집합 $\{w_1,\dots,w_k\}$의 span과 같다.

EXAM 1.32 계산 틀: "span에 속하나?" "전체를 생성하나?" How to compute with spans

(c) Is $2+3x+x^2\in\operatorname{span}(1,1+x,(1+x)^2)\subseteq\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq2}$? Solve for $a,b,c$:

$2+3x+x^2=a\cdot1+b(1+x)+c(1+x)^2=(a+b+c)+(b+2c)x+cx^2.$ Compare coefficients: $a+b+c=2$, $b+2c=3$, $c=1$ $\Rightarrow$ $c=1$, $b=1$, $a=0$.

Thus $2+3x+x^2=0\cdot1+(1+x)+(1+x)^2$ is a l.c. of $1,1+x,(1+x)^2$. So YES.

(a) $\operatorname{span}\left(\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}\right)=\mathbb{R}^2$. WTS: $\forall\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix}\in\mathbb{R}^2$, $\exists a,b$ with $a+b=x$, $a-b=y$. Elimination gives $a=\frac{x+y}{2}$, $b=\frac{x-y}{2}$. $\square$

(b) $\operatorname{span}(e_1,e_2)=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a\\b\\0\end{bmatrix}: a,b\in\mathbb{R}\right\}\not\ni\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{bmatrix}$, so $\operatorname{span}(e_1,e_2)\subsetneq\mathbb{R}^3$.

요령: 미지 계수로 일차결합을 세우고 → 성분(계수) 비교 → 연립일차방정식. 해가 있으면 속함, 모순이면 안 속함. "전체를 생성"하려면 임의의 벡터에 대해 해가 있음을 보인다.

PROP 1.33 span(S)는 S를 포함하는 가장 작은 부분공간 span(S) is the smallest subspace containing S

Let $V$ be a vector space. For every $S\subseteq V$, $\operatorname{span}(S)$ is the smallest subspace of $V$ containing $S$; i.e., $\operatorname{span}(S)$ is a subspace, $S\subseteq\operatorname{span}(S)$, and if $W\subseteq V$ is a subspace with $S\subseteq W$, then $\operatorname{span}(S)\subseteq W$. Consequently $\operatorname{span}(S)=\bigcap_{W\subseteq V \text{ subsp},\ S\subseteq W}W$.

증명 (노트는 FIS Thm 1.5 참조 — 직접 쓰면 이렇게)

If $S=\varnothing$, then $\operatorname{span}(S)=\{0\}$, which is a subspace contained in every subspace. Assume $S\neq\varnothing$.

Subspace: pick $w\in S$; $0=0\cdot w$ is a l.c. of $S$, so $0\in\operatorname{span}(S)$. If $u=\sum_{i=1}^k a_iw_i$ and $v=\sum_{j=1}^l b_jw'_j$ with $w_i,w'_j\in S$, then $u+v$ is a l.c. of the finite set $\{w_1,\dots,w_k,w'_1,\dots,w'_l\}\subseteq S$, and $cu=\sum(ca_i)w_i$; both lie in $\operatorname{span}(S)$.

Contains $S$: $w=1\cdot w$ for $w\in S$.

Smallest: if $W$ is a subspace with $S\subseteq W$, each $a_iw_i\in W$ by condition (3) and their finite sum is in $W$ by condition (2) (induction on $k$), so $\operatorname{span}(S)\subseteq W$. $\square$

DEF 1.35 · 1.37 일차종속·일차독립 Linear dependence and independence

(1) $v_1,\dots,v_n\in V$ are linearly dependent (l.d.) if $\exists(a_1,\dots,a_n)\in\mathbb{R}^n\setminus\{0\}$ s.t. $\sum_{i=1}^n a_iv_i=0$.

(2) A subset $S\subseteq V$ is l.d. if $\exists T=\{v_1,\dots,v_k\}\subseteq S$ with $T\neq\varnothing$ such that $v_1,\dots,v_k$ are l.d. as in (1).

(1′) $v_1,\dots,v_n$ are linearly independent (l.i.) if they are not l.d., i.e. $\sum_{i=1}^n a_iv_i=0\Rightarrow a_i=0\ \forall i$.

(2′) $S\subseteq V$ is l.i. if it is not l.d. (every finite subset of $S$ is l.i.). By definition, $\varnothing$ is l.i.

$\mathbb{R}^n\setminus\{0\}$ = "계수가 모두 0은 아님"(어떤 $a_j\neq0$). "모두 0이 아님"이 아니다. 서로 다른 $v_i$들로 된 집합 $\{v_1,\dots,v_n\}$이 l.d. $\iff$ 리스트 $v_1,\dots,v_n$이 l.d.

Ex 1.36. (1) If $v_i=v_j$ for some $i\neq j$, then $v_1,\dots,v_n$ are l.d.: $0=1\cdot v_i-1\cdot v_j$. (2) If $v_i=0$ for some $i$, they are l.d.: $0=1\cdot v_i$. (3) $\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}2\\2\end{bmatrix}$ are l.d.: $0=2\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}-1\cdot\begin{bmatrix}2\\2\end{bmatrix}$. (4) $e_1,e_2,\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$ are l.d. ($0=1e_1+1e_2-1\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix}$), but $e_1,e_2$ are l.i.

Ex 1.38. $e_1,\dots,e_n\in\mathbb{R}^n$ ($(e_i)_{j1}=\delta_{ij}$) are l.i.; $1,x,\dots,x^n\in\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$ are l.i.; $\sin x,\cos x\in\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})$ are l.i.

l.i. 증명 틀: $e_1,\dots,e_n$ (강의노트)

Suppose $0=\sum_{i=1}^n a_ie_i$. Then $0=\begin{bmatrix}a_1\\0\\\vdots\\0\end{bmatrix}+\cdots+\begin{bmatrix}0\\\vdots\\0\\a_n\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}a_1\\\vdots\\a_n\end{bmatrix}$, so $a_1=\cdots=a_n=0$. Hence $e_1,\dots,e_n$ are l.i. $\square$

항상 "Suppose $\sum a_iv_i=0$."으로 시작해서 "모든 $a_i=0$"으로 끝낸다.

PROP 1.39 – LEM 1.44 일차종속과 "중복(redundant)" 벡터 Dependence vs. redundancy

Prop 1.39. If $S_1\subseteq S_2\subseteq V$ and $S_1$ is l.d., then $S_2$ is l.d.   Cor 1.40. If $S_2$ is l.i., then $S_1$ is l.i. (contrapositive)

Lem 1.41. Let $S\subseteq V$, $v\in S$. If $v\in\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})$, then $S$ is l.d.

Prop 1.42. Let $v\in S$. Then $\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})=\operatorname{span}(S)\iff v\in\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})$.

Prop 1.43. $S$ is l.d. iff $\exists v\in S$ s.t. $\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})=\operatorname{span}(S)$. (Such $v$ is a "redundant" vector: removing it does not change the span.)

Lem 1.44. Let $S\subseteq V$ be l.i. and $v\in V$. If $v\notin\operatorname{span}(S)$, then $S\cup\{v\}$ is l.i.

증명: Prop 1.39 / Lem 1.41

1.39. $S_1$ l.d. $\Rightarrow$ $\exists T=\{v_1,\dots,v_k\}\subseteq S_1$ ($T\neq\varnothing$) with $v_1,\dots,v_k$ l.d. Since $T\subseteq S_1\subseteq S_2$, $S_2$ is l.d. via $T$ by definition. $\square$

1.41. $v\in\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})$, so $\exists T=\{w_1,\dots,w_k\}\subseteq S\setminus\{v\}$ with $v=\sum_{i=1}^k a_iw_i$ for some $a_i\in\mathbb{R}$. Then $a_1w_1+\cdots+a_kw_k+(-1)v=0$ with the coefficient $-1\neq0$, so $w_1,\dots,w_k,v$ are l.d. Hence $S$ is l.d. because $\{w_1,\dots,w_k,v\}\subseteq S$. $\square$

(If $S\setminus\{v\}=\varnothing$, then $v\in\operatorname{span}(\varnothing)=\{0\}$, so $v=0\in S$ and $S$ is l.d. by Ex 1.36(2).)

증명: Prop 1.42 (강의노트)

($\Rightarrow$) $\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})=\operatorname{span}(S)\supseteq S\ni v$.

($\Leftarrow$) $v\in S$ and $v\in\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})$ $\Rightarrow$ $S=(S\setminus\{v\})\cup\{v\}\subseteq\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})$, which is a subspace, so Prop 1.33 gives $\operatorname{span}(S)\subseteq\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})$. Also $S\setminus\{v\}\subseteq S\subseteq\operatorname{span}(S)$, so by Prop 1.33 $\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})\subseteq\operatorname{span}(S)$. Thus $\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})=\operatorname{span}(S)$. $\square$

증명: Prop 1.43 (⇒는 노트에 없음 — 직접 쓰면 이렇게)

($\Leftarrow$) If $\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})=\operatorname{span}(S)$, then $v\in S\subseteq\operatorname{span}(S)=\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v\})$, so $S$ is l.d. by Lem 1.41.

($\Rightarrow$) $S$ l.d. $\Rightarrow$ there are distinct $v_1,\dots,v_k\in S$ and $(a_1,\dots,a_k)\neq0$ with $\sum a_iv_i=0$. Pick $j$ with $a_j\neq0$; then $v_j=\sum_{i\neq j}\left(-\frac{a_i}{a_j}\right)v_i\in\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v_j\})$ (if $k=1$ this says $v_j=0\in\operatorname{span}(\cdot)$). By Prop 1.42, $\operatorname{span}(S\setminus\{v_j\})=\operatorname{span}(S)$. $\square$

증명: Lem 1.44 ($S$ 유한일 때, 귀류법 — 강의노트)

Let $S=\{w_1,\dots,w_n\}$. Since $v\notin\operatorname{span}(S)$, $v\notin S$, so $S\cup\{v\}=\{w_1,\dots,w_n,v\}$. Assume by contradiction that $w_1,\dots,w_n,v$ are l.d. Then $\exists(a_1,\dots,a_n,b)\in\mathbb{R}^{n+1}\setminus\{0\}$ s.t. $(\ast)\ \sum_{i=1}^n a_iw_i+bv=0$.

If $b=0$, then $(\ast)$ gives $\sum a_iw_i=0$ with $(a_1,\dots,a_n)\in\mathbb{R}^n\setminus\{0\}$, so $S$ is l.d. — contradiction to $S$ l.i.

Thus $b\neq0$, and $(\ast)$ gives $v=\sum_{i=1}^n\left(-\frac{a_i}{b}\right)w_i\in\operatorname{span}(S)$ — contradiction to $v\notin\operatorname{span}(S)$. Hence $S\cup\{v\}$ is l.i. $\square$

§1.4 Basis · 기저

DEF 1.45 · EXAM 1.46–1.47 기저와 기본 예 Basis

A basis $\beta=(v_1,\dots,v_n)\in V^n$ of $V$ is a l.i. list that also spans $V$. (A basis $\beta\subseteq V$ is a l.i. subset that spans $V$; the two definitions agree for $(v_1,\dots,v_n)$ and $\{v_1,\dots,v_n\}$.)

(1) $\varnothing$ is a basis of $\{0\}$. (2) The standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^n$ is $(e_1,\dots,e_n)$. (3) The standard basis of $\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$ is $(1,x,\dots,x^n)$. (4) $(1,x+1,(x+1)^2)$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq2}$. $\{x^n:n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq0}\}$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R}[x]$ (infinite).

기저는 유일하지 않다((3)과 (4)). $v_1,\dots,v_n$이 l.i.이면 그 자체로 $\operatorname{span}(v_1,\dots,v_n)$의 기저. 기저 = 공간을 생성하는 최소 부분집합(하나라도 빼면 span이 줄어듦, Prop 1.43).

증명: 표준기저 $(e_1,\dots,e_n)$은 ℝⁿ의 기저 (강의노트)

$\beta$ is l.i. by Ex 1.38(1). Also $\operatorname{span}(\beta)=\{a_1e_1+\cdots+a_ne_n: a_i\in\mathbb{R}\}=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a_1\\\vdots\\a_n\end{bmatrix}: a_i\in\mathbb{R}\right\}=\mathbb{R}^n$, so $\beta$ spans $\mathbb{R}^n$. Therefore $\beta$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R}^n$. $\square$

PROP 1.48 기저 ⟺ 표현의 유일성 Basis iff unique representation

$v_1,\dots,v_n\in V$ is a basis iff for every $w\in V$ there exists a unique l.c. $w=a_1v_1+\cdots+a_nv_n$.

증명 (강의노트)

($\Rightarrow$) Assume $v_1,\dots,v_n$ is a basis. Then $w\in V=\operatorname{span}(v_1,\dots,v_n)$, so $\exists(a_1,\dots,a_n)\in\mathbb{R}^n$ with $w=\sum a_iv_i$. If also $w=\sum b_iv_i$, then $0=\sum a_iv_i-\sum b_iv_i=\sum(a_i-b_i)v_i$. Since $v_1,\dots,v_n$ are l.i., $a_i-b_i=0$, i.e. $a_i=b_i$ for all $i$. Thus the l.c. is unique.

($\Leftarrow$) Every $w\in V$ is a l.c. of $v_1,\dots,v_n$, so $v_1,\dots,v_n$ span $V$. Also $0=0\cdot v_1+\cdots+0\cdot v_n$, and this l.c. is unique by assumption, so $\sum a_iv_i=0$ forces all $a_i=0$: $v_1,\dots,v_n$ are l.i. Hence they form a basis. $\square$

이 유일한 계수가 좌표 $[w]_\beta=\begin{bmatrix}a_1\\\vdots\\a_n\end{bmatrix}\in\mathbb{R}^n$ (Unit 2에서 사용).

PROP 1.49 · EXAM 1.51 유한 생성집합에서 기저 뽑기 Extracting a basis from a finite spanning set

If $V=\operatorname{span}(S)$ with $S$ finite, then $\exists\beta\subseteq S$ which is a basis for $V$ (so $V$ has a finite basis).

아이디어: $S$가 l.d.이면 Prop 1.43으로 span을 바꾸지 않는 중복 벡터를 하나 뺀다 → 반복 → $S$가 유한이므로 언젠가 l.i.가 된다(끝까지 가면 $\varnothing$, 이것도 l.i.). 노트는 이를 귀류법으로 썼다.

Ex 1.51. $S=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}3\\2\\1\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\-1\end{bmatrix}\right\}\subseteq\mathbb{R}^3$.

Notice $\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\-1\end{bmatrix}=4\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{bmatrix}+2\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{bmatrix}-\begin{bmatrix}3\\2\\1\end{bmatrix}$. Let $T:=S\setminus\left\{\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\-1\end{bmatrix}\right\}$; then $\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\-1\end{bmatrix}\in\operatorname{span}(T)$, so $\operatorname{span}(T)=\operatorname{span}(S)$ by Prop 1.42.

$\{e_1,e_2\}$ is l.i. (subset of the std basis, Cor 1.40), and $\begin{bmatrix}3\\2\\1\end{bmatrix}\notin\operatorname{span}(e_1,e_2)=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a\\b\\0\end{bmatrix}\right\}$, so $T$ is l.i. by Lem 1.44. Thus $\beta:=T$ is a basis of $\operatorname{span}(S)$.

Since $e_3=\begin{bmatrix}3\\2\\1\end{bmatrix}-3e_1-2e_2\in\operatorname{span}(\beta)$, $\mathbb{R}^3=\operatorname{span}(e_1,e_2,e_3)\subseteq\operatorname{span}(\beta)\subseteq\mathbb{R}^3$, so $\operatorname{span}(S)=\mathbb{R}^3$ and $\beta$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$.

THM 1.53 · COR 1.55 교체정리와 기저의 크기 Replacement Theorem

Replacement Thm. Let $V=\operatorname{span}(G)$ with $\#G=n\lt\infty$, and let $B\subseteq V$ be l.i. with $\#B=m\lt\infty$. Then $m\leq n$ and $\exists H\subseteq G$ s.t. $\#H=n-m$ and $B\cup H$ spans $V$.

Cor 1.55. Let $V=\operatorname{span}(\beta)$ with $\beta$ a finite basis. Then every basis $\tilde\beta$ for $V$ is finite, and $\#\tilde\beta=\#\beta$.

증명: Cor 1.55 (강의노트) + Exercise 1.54

Exercise 1.54 (if $V=\operatorname{span}(G)$ with $\#G\lt\infty$, every l.i. $B\subseteq V$ is finite): if $B$ were infinite, choose a finite subset $B'\subseteq B$ with $\#B'=\#G+1$. $B'$ is l.i. by Cor 1.40, so the Replacement Thm gives $\#B'\leq\#G$ — contradiction.

Cor 1.55. $\tilde\beta$ is a basis of $V=\operatorname{span}(\beta)$, so $\tilde\beta$ is l.i. (finite by Exercise 1.54) $\Rightarrow$ $\#\tilde\beta\leq\#\beta$ by the Replacement Thm. Switching the roles of $\beta$ and $\tilde\beta$ gives $\#\beta\leq\#\tilde\beta$. Hence $\#\beta=\#\tilde\beta$. $\square$

교체정리의 증명은 수학과 전용(비전공 분반은 진술·사용법만). 모든 벡터공간이 기저를 가진다는 사실도 수학과 전용(FIS §1.7*). 이 결과가 다음 시간 차원(dimension)의 정의로 이어진다.

자주 틀리는 포인트 · Pitfalls & Typos in the Notes

  • 공집합: $\operatorname{span}(\varnothing)=\{0\}$, $\varnothing$은 l.i., $\varnothing$은 $\{0\}$의 기저. $\{0\}$ 자체는 l.d.($1\cdot0=0$).
  • l.d.의 조건은 계수 벡터가 $0$이 아님(= 적어도 하나의 $a_j\neq0$). "모든 계수가 0이 아님"으로 쓰면 틀린다.
  • l.i. 증명 방향: "Suppose $\sum a_iv_i=0$" → … → "Hence $a_1=\cdots=a_n=0$". 거꾸로 "$a_i=0$이면 합이 0"은 아무것도 보이지 않는다(항상 참).
  • 부분공간 판정은 항상 세 조건 모두. 특히 $0_V\in W$를 빠뜨리지 말 것(공집합은 (2)(3)을 공허하게 만족하지만 부분공간이 아님).
  • 스칼라 0 vs 영벡터: $0\cdot v=0_V$ (왼쪽 $0\in\mathbb{R}$, 오른쪽 $0_V\in V$). 혼동 없으면 $0_V$를 $0$으로 쓴다.
  • 리스트 vs 집합: 리스트 $(v,v)$는 l.d., 집합 $\{v,v\}=\{v\}$는 $v\neq0$이면 l.i.
  • 다항식은 이 수업에서 함수($\mathbb{R}[x]\subseteq\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})$). 다항식이 같음 ⟺ 계수가 같음(계수 비교) — 강의도 이걸로 계산한다.
  • 노트 오타 Ex 1.25 힌트 $\deg(fg)=\deg(f)\deg(g)$ → 올바르게는 $\deg(f)+\deg(g)$.
  • 노트 오타 Unit 1.3 노트의 Motivation $W=\{(a,-a,b)\}$ → 강의(Lecture 5)의 $W=\{(a,a,b): a,b\in\mathbb{R}\}$가 맞다($a(1,1,0)+b(0,0,1)$).
  • 노트 오타 Def 1.2 "$\mathbb{R}^3:=\{(a,b,c): a,b,c\in\mathbb{R}^3\}$" → $a,b,c\in\mathbb{R}$. Def 1.4(2) "$\forall(x_1,\dots,x_n)\in\mathbb{R}$" → $\in\mathbb{R}^n$. Unit 1.2 노트 "$a+c=b+c\Rightarrow a=c$" → $a=b$.

예상 문제와 모범답안 · Practice Problems with Model Answers

노트에서 "Exercise" 또는 "Pf: Exercise"로 남긴 것 위주입니다. 먼저 영어로 답을 써 본 뒤 펼쳐 보세요.

P1 Exercise 1.27(a) 우함수 Even functions form a subspace

Prove that $E:=\{f\in\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R}): f(x)=f(-x)\ \forall x\in\mathbb{R}\}$ is a subspace of $\mathcal{F}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})$.

모범답안

We check the Subspace Criterion. (1) The zero function $0$ satisfies $0(x)=0=0(-x)$ for all $x$, so $0\in E$. (2) Let $f,g\in E$. For all $x$, $(f+g)(-x)=f(-x)+g(-x)=f(x)+g(x)=(f+g)(x)$, so $f+g\in E$. (3) Let $c\in\mathbb{R}$, $f\in E$. For all $x$, $(cf)(-x)=cf(-x)=cf(x)=(cf)(x)$, so $cf\in E$. Hence $E$ is a subspace. $\square$

P2 Exercise 1.27(b) 한 점에서 0인 다항식 Polynomials vanishing at a

Fix $a\in\mathbb{R}$, $n\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq0}$. Prove that $W:=\{f\in\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}: f(a)=0\}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$.

모범답안

(1) The zero polynomial $0\in\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$ and $0(a)=0$, so $0\in W$. (2) Let $f,g\in W$. Then $f+g\in\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$ (it is a subspace) and $(f+g)(a)=f(a)+g(a)=0+0=0$, so $f+g\in W$. (3) Let $c\in\mathbb{R}$, $f\in W$. Then $cf\in\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$ and $(cf)(a)=c\,f(a)=c\cdot0=0$, so $cf\in W$. Hence $W$ is a subspace. $\square$

P3 Exercise 1.27(c) 대칭행렬 Symmetric matrices

Prove that $\{A\in M_n(\mathbb{R}): A_{ij}=A_{ji}\ \forall 1\leq i,j\leq n\}$ is a subspace of $M_n(\mathbb{R})$.

모범답안

Call the set $S$. (1) The zero matrix $O$ has $O_{ij}=0=O_{ji}$, so $O\in S$. (2) For $A,B\in S$: $(A+B)_{ij}=A_{ij}+B_{ij}=A_{ji}+B_{ji}=(A+B)_{ji}$, so $A+B\in S$. (3) For $c\in\mathbb{R}$, $A\in S$: $(cA)_{ij}=cA_{ij}=cA_{ji}=(cA)_{ji}$, so $cA\in S$. Hence $S$ is a subspace. $\square$

P4 Ex 1.25 $\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$은 부분공간 Polynomials of degree at most n

Prove that $\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}[x]$.

모범답안

(1) $0=\sum_{i=0}^n0\cdot x^i\in\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$. (2) $\sum_{i=0}^n a_ix^i+\sum_{i=0}^n b_ix^i=\sum_{i=0}^n(a_i+b_i)x^i\in\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$. (3) $c\sum_{i=0}^n a_ix^i=\sum_{i=0}^n(ca_i)x^i\in\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$. By the Subspace Criterion, $\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq n}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}[x]$. $\square$

P5 Lecture 5 Exercise W = {(a, a, b)} is a subspace of ℝ³

Check that $W=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a\\a\\b\end{bmatrix}: a,b\in\mathbb{R}\right\}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$.

모범답안 (두 가지)

Direct. (1) $a=b=0$ gives $0\in W$. (2) $\begin{bmatrix}a\\a\\b\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}a'\\a'\\b'\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}a+a'\\a+a'\\b+b'\end{bmatrix}\in W$. (3) $c\begin{bmatrix}a\\a\\b\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}ca\\ca\\cb\end{bmatrix}\in W$. $\square$

Via span. $W=\left\{a\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{bmatrix}+b\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{bmatrix}\right\}=\operatorname{span}\left(\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{bmatrix}\right)$, which is a subspace by Prop 1.33. $\square$

P6 Lecture 6 Exercise If v₂ = cv₁, then v₁, v₂ are l.d.

모범답안

$c\cdot v_1+(-1)\cdot v_2=cv_1-cv_1=0$, and $(c,-1)\in\mathbb{R}^2\setminus\{0\}$ since $-1\neq0$. Hence $v_1,v_2$ are linearly dependent. $\square$

P7 Ex 1.38(2)(3) 1, x, …, xⁿ are l.i.; sin x, cos x are l.i.

모범답안

$1,x,\dots,x^n$. Suppose $f(x):=a_0+a_1x+\cdots+a_nx^n=0$ for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$. Then $f(0)=a_0=0$. Differentiating $k$ times, $f^{(k)}(x)=0$ for all $x$, and $f^{(k)}(0)=k!\,a_k$, so $a_k=0$ for $k=1,\dots,n$. Hence $1,x,\dots,x^n$ are l.i. $\square$ (계수 비교로 "the zero polynomial has all coefficients 0"이라고 써도 된다.)

$\sin x,\cos x$. Suppose $a\sin x+b\cos x=0$ for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$. At $x=0$: $b=0$. At $x=\pi/2$: $a=0$. Hence $\sin x,\cos x$ are l.i. $\square$

P8 Ex 1.46(4) (1, x+1, (x+1)²) is a basis of ℝ[x]≤2

모범답안

Let $a+bx+cx^2\in\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq2}$ and solve $a+bx+cx^2=\alpha\cdot1+\beta(x+1)+\gamma(x+1)^2=(\alpha+\beta+\gamma)+(\beta+2\gamma)x+\gamma x^2$. Comparing coefficients: $\gamma=c$, $\beta+2\gamma=b$, $\alpha+\beta+\gamma=a$, which has the unique solution $\gamma=c$, $\beta=b-2c$, $\alpha=a-b+c$.

So every element of $\mathbb{R}[x]_{\leq2}$ is a unique l.c. of $1,x+1,(x+1)^2$; by Prop 1.48 they form a basis. (Equivalently: existence ⇒ spanning; taking $a=b=c=0$ gives only $\alpha=\beta=\gamma=0$ ⇒ l.i.) $\square$

검산: $(a-b+c)+(b-2c)(x+1)+c(x+1)^2=a+bx+cx^2$ ✓. (3)의 $(1,x,\dots,x^n)$도 P7 + "모든 다항식이 $\sum a_ix^i$ 꼴"로 같은 방식.

P9 계산 연습 Span membership & independence in ℝ³

(a) Is $(1,2,3)\in\operatorname{span}((1,0,1),(0,1,1))$? Is $(1,2,4)$?

(b) Are $(1,1,0),(0,1,1),(1,0,1)$ linearly independent? Are $(1,0,1),(0,1,1),(1,1,2)$?

모범답안

(a) $a(1,0,1)+b(0,1,1)=(a,b,a+b)$. For $(1,2,3)$: $a=1$, $b=2$, $a+b=3$ ✓, so YES: $(1,2,3)=(1,0,1)+2(0,1,1)$. For $(1,2,4)$: $a=1$, $b=2$ forces $a+b=3\neq4$, so NO.

(b) Suppose $a(1,1,0)+b(0,1,1)+c(1,0,1)=(a+c,a+b,b+c)=0$. Then $c=-a$, $b=-a$, and $b+c=-2a=0$, so $a=b=c=0$: l.i. For the second list, $(1,0,1)+(0,1,1)-(1,1,2)=0$ is a nontrivial l.c., so they are l.d.

P10 짧은 증명 모음 Cor 1.40, Rmk 1.29(1)

모범답안

Cor 1.40. Suppose $S_1\subseteq S_2$ and $S_2$ is l.i. If $S_1$ were l.d., then $S_2$ would be l.d. by Prop 1.39, a contradiction. Hence $S_1$ is l.i. $\square$

Rmk 1.29(1). Induction on $m$. $m=1$ is clear and $m=2$ is Prop 1.28. If $W_1\cap\cdots\cap W_{m-1}$ is a subspace, then $W_1\cap\cdots\cap W_m=(W_1\cap\cdots\cap W_{m-1})\cap W_m$ is an intersection of two subspaces, hence a subspace by Prop 1.28. $\square$
